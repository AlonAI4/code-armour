Cheapest tax fix everywhere: sell through a Merchant of Record (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, app stores). Stripe Tax only calculates; you still register and file.

## India (Med; High with kids or digital sales)
- DPDP Act 2023 + Rules (notified Nov 2025) [V]: most duties start ~May 2027. Reaches foreign devs offering services in India [K]. No general small-business exemption [K].
- Children = under 18, verifiable parental consent; no tracking or targeted ads at children [V/K].
- Breach: users "without delay" + Data Protection Board within 72h [V]. Fines up to ₹250 crore [K].
- Tax: 18% GST on online digital services, foreign seller registers from the FIRST sale [V]. Equalisation levies abolished [V].
- AI: IT Rules amendment from 20 Feb 2026: visible label + permanent provenance metadata on AI-generated media; unlawful content removed within 3h [V].
- UGC: grievance officer named, acknowledge 24h, resolve 15 days [K].
- Must do: label AI output; no under-18 accounts without parental consent; Merchant of Record.

## China (High barrier, low practical exposure: site likely blocked)
- PIPL: foreign processors targeting China need a local representative; separate consent for sensitive data and transfers; under 14 = parental consent [K].
- ICP licence needs a Chinese entity [K]. Games need NPPA approval via a Chinese publisher [V].
- Minors: games 3h/week (Fri–Sun 8–9pm), real-name verification [V].
- AI: generative AI measures (2023) need security assessment + algorithm filing; labelling rules from 1 Sep 2025 (visible + metadata) [V].
- Must do: don't target China.

## Indonesia (Med)
- PDP Law in force Oct 2024, reaches abroad [V]; regulator not set up as of early 2026 [V]. Breach 3×24h; under 18 parental consent [K].
- PP 17/2025 "PP Tunas" from 1 Mar 2026 [V]: all online services set a minimum age (13+ low risk, 16+ high risk), verify age, abuse reporting, NO commercial profiling of children.
- PSE registration required for foreign operators; Steam/PayPal were blocked in 2022 for not registering [K].
- Tax: VAT collector only above IDR 600m/yr or 12,000 users/yr [V].
- Must do: state minimum age + reporting link.

## Japan (Low–Med)
- APPI reaches foreign operators; no small-business exemption; opt-in for sensitive data and transfers abroad [K/V]. Breach: first report ~3–5 days, final 30 days (60 if malicious) [V].
- Games: "kompu gacha" (pay to complete a set) banned since 2012; disclose drop rates [V].
- Commerce: Specified Commercial Transactions Act: seller name, address, phone + final confirmation screen [K]. A teen shouldn't publish a home address, so use a Merchant of Record.
- Tax: register above JPY 10m [K]. AI Promotion Act (2025), no penalties [V].
- Must do: privacy notice, no kompu gacha, MoR.

## South Korea (Med; High with loot boxes)
- PIPA reaches abroad; under 14 = guardian consent you must actually verify [V]; breach 72h [K].
- Games: loot-box odds per item mandatory since Mar 2024; up to 3× punitive damages [V]. GRAC rating (app-store self-rating counts) [K].
- AI Basic Act from 22 Jan 2026: tell users AI is used; label realistic generated output [V].
- Tax: 10% VAT, no threshold for foreign sellers [K]. Ads need opt-in + "(광고)" (advertisement) marker [K].
- Must do: exact loot-box odds, label AI, avoid under-14s or verify guardian consent.

## Vietnam (Med; games barred)
- PDPL in force 1 Jan 2026 [V]; micro businesses largely exempt from heavy duties [V]. Age 7+ child AND parent consent [K]. Breach 72h [K].
- Decree 147/2024: foreign companies can't offer online games to Vietnamese users without a local company; under-18 playtime limits [V].
- AI Law in force 1 Mar 2026, risk-based, covers foreign providers [V]; labelling detail unverified.
- Tax: ~5% VAT + 5% CIT, no threshold [K].
- Must do: don't target games at Vietnam; privacy notice.

## Philippines (Low–Med)
- Data Privacy Act; breach 72h to NPC + users [V].
- Internet Transactions Act fully in force Jun 2025, can reach foreign sellers targeting PH [V].
- Tax: 12% VAT on foreign digital services above PHP 3m/yr [V].
- Must do: privacy notice, 72h breach plan.

## Pakistan (Low)
- No comprehensive privacy law yet [V]. PECA 2016 (amended 2025) on content [K]. Stripe not available to PK-based sellers [K].
- Must do: basic privacy notice.

## Bangladesh (Low)
- Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 published Nov 2025: explicit consent, stricter for sensitive data and transfers [V]; reach, ages, breach deadline unverified.
- Must do: privacy notice + consent.

## Australia (Med)
- Privacy Act: businesses under A$3m turnover exempt [V], BUT the statutory privacy tort (from 10 Jun 2025) applies to everyone [V/K]. Children's Online Privacy Code due by 10 Dec 2026 [V].
- Under-16 social media ban from 10 Dec 2025; online games, messaging, education excluded [V]. An app whose main purpose is social could be caught [K].
- Tax: 10% GST only at A$75k+ AU sales [K]. Spam Act: consent, sender ID, unsubscribe [K].
- Accessibility: Disability Discrimination Act applies to websites [K].
- Must do: Spam-Act emails; no social feed for under-16s.

## Singapore (Low–Med)
- PDPA applies, no small-business exemption; parental consent under 13 suggested [K]. Breach: PDPC within 3 days of deciding it's notifiable [V].
- Online Safety Commission + statutory torts from 29 Jun 2026 for platforms hosting harmful user content [V].
- Tax: GST only above S$1m global AND S$100k SG sales [V].
- Must do: privacy notice, 3-day breach plan, takedown/report if UGC.

## Cross-cutting (ranked)
1. Age gate. Safe default: no under-18 accounts or data collection without parental consent.
2. Label AI output, visibly + in metadata (China, India, Korea, Vietnam).
3. Loot boxes: per-item odds (Korea); never kompu gacha (Japan).
4. Taxes: Merchant of Record (India, Korea, Vietnam have no threshold).
5. Don't target China, or Vietnam for games.
6. Breach clocks: SG 3 days; IN/PH/KR/VN 72h; ID 3×24h; JP 3–5 days.

Unverified: China incident deadlines, Bangladesh details, Pakistan digital tax, Vietnam AI labelling, Australia AI chatbot codes, PH child age, Japan JCT threshold, Korea VAT threshold and AI fine grace period.
