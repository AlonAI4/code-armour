# Dominican Republic (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Note: only search snippets read; dgii.gov.do and indotel.gob.do not opened. [V-sec] = news/law-firm text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 172-13 (13 Dec 2013) protects personal data in public and private databases; clym/ACLaw summaries say it also covers foreign companies processing data of Dominicans or using local infrastructure (secondary). Requires a legal basis (consent, contract, legal duty), purpose limitation, security measures; art. 22 covers incident handling.
- [K] No dedicated data authority exists; enforcement runs through courts, the consumer agency (Pro Consumidor) and Indotel; experts call the law outdated and ask for a new one (ECIJA, El Nuevo Diario).
- [K] Breach deadline and children's consent age: not specified. Cybercrime: Law 53-07 [V-sec].

## Kids and games
- [K] No game age or loot-box law found. Online gambling is regulated by the Casinos/Betting law [K].

## Selling
- [V-sec] Decree 30-25 (Jan 2025) would have charged ITBIS (18%) on foreign digital services (streaming, ads, cloud, games, e-books) but was repealed by Decree 107-25 soon after (Focus Gaming News, Acento). So today there is no foreign-digital ITBIS regime. 
- [K] ITBIS rate is 18%. [V-sec] Law 32-23 is about electronic invoicing for local taxpayers, not foreign sellers. A new attempt is plausible.
- [K] Consumer Law 358-05 applies to distance sales; no cooling-off for digital goods confirmed. Stripe supports businesses in the DR [K, not confirmed].

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law; eight AI bills sat in committees and were set to expire on 12 Jan 2026 (El Caribe). Check whether re-filed.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy policy in Spanish, consent, deletion path; little risk without a local presence.
- Sell as normal; no VAT registration needed now. Use a Merchant of Record if ITBIS returns.
- Keep a report/remove path for chat.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] AI bills may be re-filed in the new legislature; the foreign-digital ITBIS idea may return.
