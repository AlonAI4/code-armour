# Ecuador (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. VAT is mostly withheld by card issuers if you don't register.
Note: SRI and Superintendencia sites not fetched. Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales (LOPDP), published 26 May 2021; Regulation 13 Nov 2023 (DLA Piper). Fully enforceable since May 2023 [K].
- [K] Reaches foreign controllers who offer goods/services to people in Ecuador or monitor them (Art. 2). Not confirmed today.
- [V-sec] Breach notice: authority within 5 days; data subjects within 3 days of becoming aware of the risk; processor tells controller within 2 days (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Children: teens can consent from 15; under 15 needs parent/guardian (search snippet; law text not read).
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad allowed with adequacy, contract clauses, binding corporate rules, or explicit consent.
- [V-sec] Fines: 0.1-0.7% of turnover (minor), 0.7-1% (serious). Regulator: Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or loot-box law found. Children's Code (Código de la Niñez) protects under-18s generally; avoid collecting kid data without a parent.

## Selling
- [V-sec] IVA (VAT) is 15% since April 2024 and applies to digital services from non-resident providers; no threshold (Kintsugi, PayPro).
- [V-sec] Unregistered foreign providers: the card issuer / payment processor withholds the IVA from the payment. Or register with SRI (RUC) and charge it yourself. SRI keeps a public list (catastro) of foreign digital providers.
- [K] Consumer law (Ley Orgánica de Defensa del Consumidor) + e-commerce rules: clear price, terms, a withdrawal right for distance sales; length for digital items unconfirmed.
- Practical: a Merchant of Record avoids double-tax mess for customers.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law: the National Assembly archived the AI bill on 15 Sep 2026 (asambleanacional.gob.ec, via search snippet). A separate bill on AI and children existed (2024); status unclear.
- [K] No platform-registration or local-representative rule found for small foreign apps.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Spanish privacy notice; parent consent (or block) under 15; delete/export on request.
- Breach plan: tell users within 3 days, authority within 5.
- Sales: use a Merchant of Record, or accept that banks withhold 15% IVA.
- Label AI features anyway (good practice, not law).
