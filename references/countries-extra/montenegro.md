# Montenegro (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low today. Med on paper from 19 Mar 2027, when a GDPR-style law reaches foreign apps and requires a local representative.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Today: Law on Protection of Personal Data (2008, last amended Jul 2024, Official Gazette 77/2024). It is old-style. You register your controller and each database with the Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information (AZLP), there is no general breach-notice duty, and fines are EUR 500-20,000 (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] New law: a 106-article GDPR-style Law on Personal Data Protection was reportedly adopted on 4 Sep 2026. It enters into force about 19 Sep 2026 and applies from about 19 Mar 2027 (PPC Land, 20 Sep 2026). The government's own document library lists the bill (Predlog zakona) on 10 Aug 2026 (gov.me). The final gazette text was not read.
- [V-sec] Under the new law (PPC Land summary): it reaches foreign companies that offer goods or services to people in Montenegro or monitor them (Art. 3). They must name a representative in Montenegro (Art. 28), with a narrow exemption for occasional, low-risk processing. Children can consent alone from 16 (Art. 9). Breaches go to the AZLP within 72 hours (Art. 34). Fines are up to EUR 2M or 4% of worldwide turnover (Art. 88).
- [V-sec] Transfers: under the current law, transfers to the EU/EEA and EU-adequate countries need no AZLP approval; others generally do. Under the new law, EU standard contractual clauses stay switched off until EU membership (Art. 104).
- [V-sec] Cookies: no specific cookie law. Electronic marketing needs prior consent (Law on Electronic Trade). Using traffic or location data for marketing needs consent (Electronic Communications Law 100/2024).
- [K] EU adequacy: none. Montenegro is an EU candidate and uses the euro.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is a licensed state concession.

## Selling
- [V-sec] New Consumer Protection Law: Official Gazette 12/2026, in force 14 Feb 2026. It has a withdrawal right for distance contracts, clear total-price and withdrawal info before purchase, and refunds by the same payment method. Missing info extends the withdrawal period (GTAI). The exact number of days was not confirmed; [K] the EU standard is 14 days.
- [V-sec] VAT (PDV) 21%. Several tax vendors say foreign B2C sellers of e-services register from the first sale (no threshold), via form PR-PDV or a fiscal representative. VAT amendments adopted 2 Feb 2026 align place-of-supply rules with the EU VAT Directive (Kintsugi, Anrok, BDK Advokati). Sources disagree on details.
- Practical: Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. EU accession means EU AI Act-style rules are likely later.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Plan for March 2027: privacy policy, consent for under-16s, 72-hour breach plan. If you deliberately target Montenegro, check whether you need a local representative.
- Merchant of Record for paid sales.
- Show price, withdrawal rules and your contact details before checkout.

## Upcoming
