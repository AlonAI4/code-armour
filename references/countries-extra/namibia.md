# Namibia (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (no data protection law in force, no digital-services VAT yet).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law in force. A Data Protection Bill (drafts since 2013/2023) was due to be tabled in Sept 2025; no record found of it passing (The Brief, The Namibian, NAMPA, Chambers 2026).
- [K] Constitution Art. 13 protects privacy. Follow GDPR-style basics anyway.
- [K] No cookie, breach-notice, localisation or children's-consent rule in force.

## Kids and games
- [K] Child Care and Protection Act 2015: a child is under 18. No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is licensed; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Electronic Transactions Act 4 of 2019: in force 16 Mar 2020 except s.20, Chapter 4 (consumer protection) and Chapter 5; s.20 and Chapter 5 started 15 Jun 2026 (GN 182/2026). Chapter 4 is still not in force (LAC annotated statute; NamibLII summary).
- [V-sec] When Chapter 4 starts: online sellers must show set information (s.34) and consumers get a 7-day cooling-off (goods: 7 days from receipt; services: 7 days from agreement), only return shipping chargeable (s.35) (LAC text).
- [V-sec] VAT on imported digital services: announced in the 2025/26 budget ("being finalised"), 15% under discussion, maybe collected via banks; no law or start date found as of Dec 2025 reporting (VATCalc, The Brief, The Namibian, KPMG).
- Practical: no VAT duty now; a Merchant of Record covers you when it arrives.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.
- [V-sec] ETA Chapter 6 (in force): hosts/website services get safe harbour if they remove unlawful material "expeditiously" after a written take-down notice and have a designated agent (s.52-54) (LAC text).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Little Namibia-specific law applies today. Follow your home/GDPR-style baseline.
- Have a take-down contact if you host user content.
- Write a fair refund policy (7-day cooling-off is coming).
- Watch for the data protection law and digital VAT.
