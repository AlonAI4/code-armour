# Qatar (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med if your app targets kids (strict children's rules); Low otherwise. No VAT.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Privacy Protection Law, Law No. 13 of 2016 (PDPPL). Regulator: National Cyber Governance and Assurance Affairs, National Cyber Security Agency (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Applies to electronically processed personal data; secondary sources say it affects foreign businesses handling Qatari users' data.
- [V-sec] Breach: notify regulator and affected people as soon as possible, at most 72 hours.
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad allowed; controllers shouldn't block data flows unless processing breaks the law.
- [V-sec] Fines up to QAR 5M.
- [V-sec] "Special" data (health, religion, children, ethnicity, crime...) needs prior permission from the competent authority (Mondaq/Clym summaries). [K] how this is applied in practice.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Websites aimed at children must get express parent/guardian approval before processing a child's data, give parents access, and must NOT make game participation depend on giving excessive data (DLA Piper).
- [K] Gambling is banned (Penal Code): no paid random rewards that can be cashed out; loot boxes sold for money are risky. Some games are banned/edited for content.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No VAT in force in 2026. GCC framework says 5% one day; e-invoicing law prepared, no VAT date (vatupdate, Jul 2026).
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 8/2008 + e-commerce law: Arabic info, clear prices.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law; NCSA guidelines on secure AI use (non-binding). Cybercrime Law 14/2014 punishes content harming social values, privacy or the state: moderate user content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Kids' app: parent consent, parent access, minimal data in games.
- 72-hour breach plan.
- Moderate chat/user content (religion, royal family, privacy).
- No tax action; watch for VAT.
