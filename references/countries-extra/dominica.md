# Dominica (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Not the Dominican Republic (see dominican-republic.md).
Severity: Low. No data protection law in force; no tax rule for foreign digital sellers found.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law in force as of 2025 (UWI data protection resource, Feb 2025; LawGratis, Apr 2025). No bill passed found since.
- [V-sec] Only the Constitution's privacy protection (home/property) and the Electronic Evidence Act 2010 (LawGratis).
- [V-sec] OECS started work on harmonised data protection laws for members incl. Dominica in Jan 2022; no Dominica law resulted yet (UWI).
- [K] No cookie law, no children's data age.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box, game-time or kids' online law.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 15% (Inland Revenue Division) (Expanship). VAT Act Ch. 67:08 amended 2023 and by Schedule Orders 2025-2026 (Dominica Government Printery).
- [V-sec] No rule found making foreign digital sellers register for VAT (searched Gazette listings; contents of 2025/2026 orders not read).
- [K] Consumer protection: general law; no online cooling-off right found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law found; CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap is non-binding (Jun 2026).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No local privacy law: follow your home-country rules and general good practice.
- Nothing Dominica-specific to register for. Merchant of Record still easiest if you sell.

## Upcoming
- [K] An OECS-model data protection bill may come; no date.
