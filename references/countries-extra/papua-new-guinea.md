# Papua New Guinea (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low today (no data protection law in force). Could rise fast: data, cybersecurity and social media bills are all in the works.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: ict.gov.pg news pages were seen only as search snippets (marked [V-sec]); the consultation-notice PDF was read in full.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No standalone data protection law in force as of mid-2026; Cybercrime Code Act 2016 is the closest binding law (DataGuidance; tracker).
- [V-reg] Draft Data Governance and Data Protection Bill 2026 went to public consultation; submissions closed 19 Aug 2026. It covers individual rights, children's data, breach notification, cross-border transfers and "sovereign hosting", and an interim AI framework (ict.gov.pg consultation notice).
- [V-sec] Draft fines up to PGK 1 million for businesses; biometric data only with explicit consent or legal purpose (Biometric Update, 11 Aug 2026).
- [K] No cookie rule, no children's data age in force.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] National Social Media Policy 2025 endorsed by Cabinet (NEC Decision 184/2025): age restrictions, SevisPass digital ID for social media, platform accountability. Not law (ict.gov.pg, Oct 2025).
- [V-sec] 1 Sep 2026: DICT and Office of Censorship started drafting the social media law; age limits and checks "not yet announced"; may group platforms by risk (ict.gov.pg). Earlier figures: under-16 restriction (2024), "14-16" (2025).
- [V-sec] Draft Cybersecurity Bill 2026 includes social media age verification that must not require a government ID and must offer an alternative; a Child Online Safety Commissioner would oversee it (Biometric Update).
- [K] No loot-box law.

## Selling
- [V-sec] GST 10%. Registration required when taxable supplies exceed PGK 250,000 in 12 months, including foreign contractors (PwC, Aug 2026; KPMG 2019).
- [V-sec] No special rule found for foreign sellers of digital services to PNG consumers.
- [V-sec] Consumer law is thin (mainly product safety); a new competition and consumer bill with misleading-conduct bans and quality guarantees is in consultation (PSDI).

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Draft Government AI Adoption Framework (Feb 2026) and draft National Sovereign Digital Transformation and AI Strategy (Mar 2026) were out for consultation; officials mention a future National AI Act (ict.gov.pg). Public-sector focus; no AI law.
- [V-sec] Earlier proposals said platforms must register in PNG and route traffic via the local internet exchange; unclear if kept (Biometric Update, Dec 2024).
- [V-sec] Cybercrime Code Act 2016 criminalises distributing pornography and other online offences.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Nothing PNG-specific to register for today.
- Follow GDPR-style basics; the PNG bill will likely look similar (rights, breach notice, children).
- Social apps: expect age checks under the coming social media law.
- Sales: you're very unlikely to hit PGK 250k; a Merchant of Record is still easiest.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Social Media Legislation: government aims to introduce before end of 2026 (May 2026 release).
- [V-reg] Data Governance and Data Protection Bill 2026, Cybersecurity Bill 2026, Verifiable Credential & Trust Services Bill 2026: consultation closed 19 Aug 2026; tabling date unknown.

## Unverified
