# Nauru (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law in force. Council of Europe (Octopus) says data protection is covered only in a limited way, as "confidentiality"; SEAP says Nauru has no data protection or privacy laws.
- [V-sec] National Digital Transformation Strategy 2025-2030 plans data protection legislation. No bill found.
- [V-sec] Cybercrime Act 2015 is criminal law (hacking, illegal access, identity misuse), not rules for how apps handle data. Communications and Broadcasting Act 2018 has confidentiality duties for telecom subscribers.
- No children's consent age for data, no breach-notice rule, no localisation rule found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No kids' online or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No VAT/GST or general sales tax (Expanship; Nauru Revenue Office statutes listed there are business tax, employment and services tax, revenue administration). Nothing for foreign digital sellers to collect.
- [K] Nauru uses the Australian dollar. No consumer refund law for online sales found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No country-specific privacy law: follow the general rules in SKILL.md.
- Watch for a data protection bill under the 2025-2030 digital strategy.
- No tax to collect on digital sales.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data protection law planned under the National Digital Transformation Strategy 2025-2030; no date.

## Unverified
