# Sierra Leone (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for privacy (no data-protection law in force); Low-Med for paid apps (GST on foreign digital services from 2026).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] No data-protection law in force yet. The Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2025 finished national validation on 7 Nov 2025; next step was Cabinet approval, then Parliament. One independent authority would cover data protection and access to information (Ministry of Information and Civic Education, 10 Nov 2025).
- [V-sec] A 2025 SierraLII article also says Sierra Leone has no data-protection regime.
- [V-sec] Sierra Leone signed the ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on personal data (2010) but has no national authority to enforce it.
- [V-sec] Cyber Security and Crime Act 2021 has child online protection and privacy provisions (SierraLII commentary). Unlawful access / data misuse can be a crime.
- Practical: no consent age, breach deadline or transfer rule yet. Keep your GDPR-style baseline.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Child Rights Act 2025 replaced the 2007 Act (Human Rights Commission report, 2026); no online-specific rules found in it.
- [K] Child = under 18. No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 1 of 2026, in force 1 Jan 2026): a non-resident making taxable supplies of digital services to consumers in Sierra Leone must register for GST, even with no physical presence, and must appoint a local representative (GST Act ss. 15(4), 100 as amended) (lookuptax).
- [K] GST rate 15%.
- [V-sec] Threshold reported as SLE 350,000 a year for non-resident digital suppliers, monthly returns (PayPro Global, a vendor; single source, not confirmed in the Act).
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Act 2020 (gazetted 3 Sep 2020) covers goods and services in trade; online sales not named but seem covered (SierraLII; secondary summary).
- [V-sec] Electronic Transactions Act (2019 bill; sources disagree on its number) has consumer sections 13-21 incl. s.17 "consumer's right of withdrawal". Period not confirmed.
- Practical: Merchant of Record; show a clear refund policy.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law or AI strategy rules found for private apps.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy: no local law yet, but a bill is moving; build to your normal baseline.
- Paid apps: a Merchant of Record handles the 2026 GST rule (registration + local representative otherwise).
- Kids: no specific rule; treat under-18s carefully.
