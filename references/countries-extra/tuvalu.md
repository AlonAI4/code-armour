# Tuvalu (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law in force. Tuvalu has no dedicated data protection legislation (SEAP digital ID overview, updated Jul 2025). UNCTAD's 2025 Pacific cyberlaw gap study also flags data protection as the main gap in the region.
- [K] The Constitution protects privacy in general terms, and telecom/sector rules may impose confidentiality. Neither gives an app developer clear duties.
- [V-sec] No bill found in 2025-2026 searches.
- No children's consent age for data, no breach-notice rule, no localisation rule found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No kids' online or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Consumption Tax Act 2008, 3% rate since 1 Jul 2009 (Consumption Tax Regulation 2009). The Act has a reverse charge on "foreign services" (s.16), which points to the local buyer paying, not a foreign seller registering (PacLII index snippets).
- [K] No special regime making foreign digital sellers register found. Tuvalu uses the Australian dollar.
- [K] No consumer refund law for online sales found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No country-specific privacy law: follow the general rules in SKILL.md (privacy notice, minimal data, secure storage).
- Watch for a future data protection bill (none found yet).
- Nothing extra needed for tax at small scale.
