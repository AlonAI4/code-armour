# Rwanda (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Med on paper (registration + data stored in Rwanda unless authorised), Low in practice for a tiny foreign app.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. 058/2021 on Protection of Personal Data and Privacy, in force 15 Oct 2021. Regulator: National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) (DLA Piper, Hogan Lovells).
- [V-sec] Applies to controllers/processors with no local presence if they process data of people in Rwanda.
- [V-sec] Controllers and processors must register with the NCSA (certificate within 30 days); application asks for your representative if any.
- [V-sec] Localisation: personal data must be stored in Rwanda, unless your NCSA registration certificate authorises storage abroad.
- [V-sec] Children under 16: parent/guardian consent, in the child's interest.
- [V-sec] Breach: NCSA within 48 hours; data subjects if high risk.
- [V-sec] Fines RWF 2-5M or 1% of turnover; criminal fines up to 5%.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is licensed; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Ministerial Order No. 004/26/10/TC, gazetted 29 Apr 2026: 18% VAT on digital services by non-residents to Rwandan consumers (streaming, software, games, e-learning...). Three-month window to register or appoint a local tax representative (Fonoa).
- [V-sec] No threshold mentioned. Treat as from sale 1.
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] National AI Policy (2023); no AI law. Rwanda moderates online speech strictly (genocide denial / "divisionism" laws), so moderate user content.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Parent consent (or block) under 16.
- 48-hour breach plan.
- Know that registration + "store in Rwanda" rules exist; if you target Rwanda specifically, register with the NCSA and request transfer authorisation.
- Merchant of Record for paid sales (new 2026 VAT).
