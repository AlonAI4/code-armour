# Tanzania (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Med if you host user content (strict online-content rules); Low for a plain site or game.
Note: tcra.go.tz PDF found but not read. Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Act 2022, in force 1 May 2023; regulator Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC), launched Apr 2024 (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] All controllers/processors must register with the PDPC (valid 5 years); the President set a 31 Dec 2024 deadline for local institutions. Whether foreign apps must register: [K] the law focuses on processing in Tanzania; unclear.
- [V-sec] Children's data is sensitive data: needs prior written consent (parent). No age given in source; [K] child = under 18.
- [V-sec] Breach: notify PDPC promptly (no fixed hours found).
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad need adequacy/safeguards and a PDPC permit.
- [V-sec] Fines up to TZS 100M; criminal fines up to TZS 5bn for companies.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box law; gambling regulated by the Gaming Board; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 18% (mainland) on B2C digital services by non-residents; simplified registration since 1 Jul 2022; no threshold for non-residents (Kintsugi, Anrok).
- [K] There is also a 2% digital service income tax on non-residents (2022). Zanzibar has its own digital VAT plans (2027, lookuptax).
- Practical: Merchant of Record, since there is no threshold.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Online Content Regulations amended 28 Jan 2025 (GN 57): wider banned-content list incl. "unethical, fabricated and AI-generated" videos/audio/pictures; duties on platform owners to remove content (Bowmans).
- [K] Running an online TV/radio/blog for Tanzanians can need a TCRA licence; X has been blocked in Tanzania since 2025.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Parent consent for kids' data; privacy notice.
- If users post content: fast report/remove; don't allow deceptive AI media of real people.
- Use a Merchant of Record for sales.
- Avoid political content hosting risks; be ready for local blocking.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Zanzibar digital services VAT (planned 2027, per lookuptax).
