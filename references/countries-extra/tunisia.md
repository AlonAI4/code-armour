# Tunisia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. Content law (Decree 54) is a real risk for chat; VAT is collected via payer withholding.
Note: no regulator site fetched (inpdp.nat.tn not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Organic Law 2004-63; regulator INPDP; explicit consent; processing must be declared to INPDP. Covers local and foreign organisations handling Tunisian data. (Signzy, DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] New bill PLO 095/2025 (132 articles) filed with parliament 5 Aug 2025 to repeal it: DPO, breach reporting, AI profiling limits, fines up to TND 200,000. (AllAfrica, Global Law Experts)
- [V-sec] CONFLICT: one site (regulations.ai) says the new law enters into force 11 Jul 2026; others say it is still a bill. Treat as pending until confirmed.
- [K] Children's consent rules, breach deadline, and transfer authorisation by INPDP: not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found. [K] Gambling is tightly restricted; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 19%. Services from non-residents used in Tunisia: VAT collected by 100% withholding at source by the payer (Note Commune 24/2014), plus possible 5% royalty tax on software. No foreign-seller registration regime. (vatcalc, Quaderno)
- [K] Consumer law 83-2000 and e-commerce law 2000-83 apply to online sales. Stripe not available to Tunisian sellers.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Decree-Law 54 of 2022: up to 5 years for spreading "false news"; used against journalists. (CPJ, ICJ)
- [K] No AI law; AI profiling limits appear only in the pending bill.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy policy, explicit consent, deletion route; parent consent for kids.
- Moderate chat for political "false news"; report button.
- Taxes: Merchant of Record or app stores; do not register yourself.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] PLO 095/2025 data-protection bill: pending (one source claims 11 Jul 2026; unconfirmed).
