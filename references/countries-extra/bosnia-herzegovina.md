# Bosnia and Herzegovina (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app. Med if you sell direct (VAT + fiscal representative).
Note: no regulator or tax-authority site read; all lines below are secondary sources or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] New Law on Protection of Personal Data adopted Jan 2025, published 28 Feb 2025, GDPR-aligned; replaces the 2006 law. Sources disagree on when it applies: one says it entered into force on publication, another says 4 Oct 2025. Treat as in force now. (CEE Legal Matters, fiscal-requirements)
- [V-sec] Parental consent for under-16s for online services; breach notice within 72h; foreign controllers processing BiH residents' data need a local representative. (CMS / Clym snippets)
- [V-sec] GDPR-level fines (up to EUR 20M / 4%) are reported, but this is the law text only; real enforcement by the Personal Data Protection Agency has been rare. [K]

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time, loot-box or social media age law found. Not searched exhaustively.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Since 2023, B2C digital services by non-residents are subject to 17% VAT. Registration threshold reported as BAM 100,000 (one source) and a fiscal representative is required; monthly filing by the 10th. Sources conflict on whether the threshold applies to non-residents, so ask a tax adviser before relying on it. (Anrok, Pay Pro Global, fiscal-requirements)
- [K] Consumer protection is split between entities (FBiH, RS, Brcko), so cooling-off rules vary. Not verified.
- [K] Stripe does not support BiH businesses; merchant of record (Paddle etc.) can sell to buyers there. Not verified.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. No platform registration rule found.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Treat BiH users like EU users for privacy; parent consent for under-16s; 72h breach plan.
- A free app has little extra exposure. If you sell, use a merchant of record to avoid fiscal-representative VAT work.
- Consider a local representative only if you have meaningful BiH user numbers (rule is real but enforcement is unproven).
