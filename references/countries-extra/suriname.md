# Suriname (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (no data protection law; VAT on foreign digital services only above a high threshold).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official law text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tracker, [K] memory. The VAT law PDF on belastingdienst.sr returned 404; dna.sr drafts seen only in search snippets.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] **No data protection law in force.** The "Wet Bescherming Privacy en Persoonsgegevens" bill (with an independent Commissioner) was sent to the National Assembly in 2018, discussed by the rapporteurs' committee 21 Jan 2021, no progress since (DataGuidance, lawgratis, dna.sr draft marked "ONTWERP").
- [V-sec] The draft would reach foreign businesses offering goods or services to people in Suriname, with consent, access/correction/deletion rights and transfer conditions. Not law.
- [K] Constitution protects privacy; no regulator.

## Kids and games
- [K] No children's data, loot-box or game-time law. Age of majority 18 [K, not confirmed]. Gambling licensed locally.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT (BTW) since 1 Jan 2023, standard 10% (S.B. 2022 No. 121). Non-resident providers of electronic services (streaming, games, apps, software, e-books) to Surinamese consumers must register and charge VAT once B2C sales pass **SRD 500,000** a year; B2B by reverse charge; local representative reportedly not required (vatcalc, Kintsugi, PayPro).
- [V-sec] No consumer protection law found in force: a 2020 private member's Consumentenbeschermingswet bill (to be overseen by an ACMS authority) has no record of passing (dna.sr).
- [K] Stripe does not support Suriname-based businesses; selling to Surinamese from abroad works (card acceptance can be patchy).

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law or national AI policy found (CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap endorsed 18 Jun 2026 covers the region).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No data law in force; keep a privacy policy and good security anyway.
- Tiny sellers are under the SRD 500,000 VAT threshold; a Merchant of Record handles it if you grow.
- Offer a fair refund policy (no statutory cooling-off found).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Privacy bill and consumer bill pending, no dates. A June 2026 headline mentioned postponement of a "new VAT system" (not read; unclear if it affects digital services).
