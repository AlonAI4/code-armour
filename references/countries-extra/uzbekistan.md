# Uzbekistan (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app. Localisation was the big barrier and was eased in Mar 2026; foreign VAT and AI labelling are new.
Note: tax.uz, lex.uz and regulator sites were not read; lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law "On Personal Data" (2019). Amendments signed 26 Mar 2026: mandatory in-country storage now only for biometric and genetic data and telecom subscriber data; other data may be stored abroad if the country is on an adequate list (49 countries approved Aug 2026), or with standard contractual clauses / binding rules, or under international standards. (Kun.uz, Legal 500, Times of Central Asia)
- [K] Consent-based; the regulator is the Personal Data Protection Agency (under the Ministry of Digital Technologies); fines apply. Foreign reach, children's age, breach deadline: not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Uzbekistan is considering restricting social media for under-16s; announced 26 Jun 2026, draft law not yet published. (Kun.uz)
- [K] No loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Law ZRU-1173 (in force 2026): foreign companies providing e-services to individuals must register for VAT, rate 12%, no threshold, via the foreign-provider portal at tax.uz, within 30 days of starting; no local company needed. (Anrok, Quaderno, KPMG Sep 2026, EY)
- [K] Stripe and PayPal are not usable for Uzbek buyers or sellers until now; the 2026 amendments aim to open this up (Apple Pay / Google Pay / PayPal coming). Merchant of record availability unverified.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] AI law No. LRU-1115 signed 21 Jan 2026: AI-generated content shared online must be labelled; bans AI uses that harm dignity, freedom or health. (Babl.ai, Kun.uz)
- [K] Platforms must remove illegal content on request; foreign sites can be blocked. Not verified.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- If selling to Uzbek individuals: register for 12% VAT from the first sale, or use a merchant of record.
- Label AI-generated output shown to users. Label your AI coach / generated images.
- Keep biometrics out of the app; any other personal data you may keep abroad, ideally with standard clauses.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Under-16 social media restriction: draft planned (no date). Apple Pay / Google Pay launch expected.
