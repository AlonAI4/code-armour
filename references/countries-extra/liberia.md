# Liberia (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (no confirmed data-protection law in force; no regulator).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No dedicated data-protection law confirmed in force. DLA Piper (updated 23 Feb 2024): "no dedicated privacy law"; only sector rules (Telecommunications Act, Central Bank e-payment and AML rules). DataGuidance: no general law and no authority.
- [V-sec] A bill ("Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2024", drafted by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications) was validated Dec 2024, submitted by President Boakai and sent to the House committee on Post and Telecommunication on a 10 June (Liberian Observer). The draft reaches anyone handling data of residents of Liberia, the ECOWAS region "or beyond".
- [V-sec] One profile cites a 25 June 2026 Liberia Internet Governance Forum statement that the President signed it. That is single-source and no enacted text or start date was found. Treat as unconfirmed (see Unverified).
- [V-sec] Liberia is bound by the ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on personal data (2010) but has set up no authority under it (DLA Piper).
- [K] Constitution Art. 16 protects privacy.
- Practical: no consent age, breach deadline or transfer rule to follow today. Keep your GDPR-style baseline; it will cover most of the bill if it lands.

## Kids and games
- [K] Children's Law 2011: child = under 18. No loot-box, game-time or children's-data rule found.

## Selling
- [V-reg] Goods and Services Tax (GST) on services rose to 13% from 1 May 2026 (telecoms 15%) under the Liberia Tax Amendment Act (approved 24 Mar 2026). The notice says nothing on foreign digital services (LRA notice, 7 Apr 2026).
- [V-sec] VAT is planned to replace GST; sources point to a 1 Jan 2027 launch, likely 18%. The 2026 budget signals taxing non-resident digital services (streaming, software, e-learning); rules not yet set (VATCalc, 14 Nov 2025).
- [K] No online refund / cooling-off law found.
- Practical: a Merchant of Record will handle VAT if the 2027 rules catch foreign sellers.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Nothing Liberia-specific to do today for privacy; keep your baseline.
- Recheck in 2027: VAT launch may include foreign digital sellers.
- Watch whether the data-protection bill was really signed (2026).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] VAT to replace GST, planned 1 Jan 2027 (~18%), possibly with a non-resident digital-services regime.
- [V-sec] Personal data protection bill: reportedly signed June 2026, unconfirmed; start date unknown.
