# Jordan (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. Content/cybercrime law is the sharper risk; sales over the VAT threshold is the main money duty.
Note: no regulator site fetched (istd.gov.jo / pdp authority not read). [V-sec] = seen in a law-firm/news/vendor page today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023: published 17 Sep 2023, in force 17 Mar 2024, transition ended 17 Mar 2025. (Clyde & Co, Clym)
- [V-sec] Reaches foreign businesses offering services to people in Jordan; explicit consent needed for sensitive data. (Clym)
- [V-sec] Data breaches must be reported to the authority; exact deadline not found. [K] children need guardian consent; cross-border transfers need adequate protection. No small-business exemption found.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Feb 2026: Cabinet formed a national committee on child online safety; options include age verification and tougher platform oversight. Not yet a law. (meatechwatch)
- [V-sec] TikTok was reported banned Dec 2022; current status [K] unclear. No loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] General Sales Tax 16%. Non-resident digital service providers can register and file on the ISTD e-commerce portal (launched 2024). (vatcalc, payproglobal)
- [V-sec] Threshold JOD 30,000 per 12 months, bi-monthly filing: one vendor page only, treat as weak. Merchant of Record or app stores avoid this.
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 7/2017 applies to online sales. Stripe is not available for Jordan-based sellers; selling to Jordanian buyers with cards works.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Cybercrime Law No. 17 of 2023 (in force 13 Sep 2023): prosecutor/court can order removal or blocking of content; platforms that ignore notices can face ad bans and bandwidth throttling after six months. (CPJ, Wikipedia)
- [V-sec] AI: only a national AI strategy 2023-2027, no AI law. (dig.watch)
- [K] No local representative rule found for small apps.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy with explicit consent for sensitive data; guardian consent or age gate for kids; a way to delete data.
- Chat/user content: report button and fast removal; the cybercrime law punishes "fake news" and insults.
- Under JOD 30,000/yr of Jordan sales: likely nothing; above it use a Merchant of Record or register for GST.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Child online-safety committee (Feb 2026) may lead to age-verification rules. No date.
