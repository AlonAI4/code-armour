# Equatorial Guinea (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app: a 2016 data law exists but its regulator has never started work; no digital VAT rule found.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory. No official text found online (Spanish only, gazette not reachable); everything here is secondary.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. 1/2016 of 22 Jul 2016 on the Protection of Personal Data. Consent is the main principle; rights of access, rectification, cancellation, objection; security duties (DataGuidance, DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Regulator: Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales; not operational, so many provisions have limited effect and there is no practical enforcement (dataprotection.africa, DataGuidance, ConsentStack).
- [V-sec] Files must be registered with the General Data Protection Registry (Art. 33). Processing beliefs, health, sex life, race or religion without express consent is a "major infringement" (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Breach duty exists but no deadline or recipient is stated (DLA Piper).
- [K] No clear foreign-reach, cross-border transfer or localisation rule found.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] No minors-specific rule found in the sources (DLA Piper, DataGuidance). [K] Civil majority is 18; get parent consent under 18 to be safe.
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Standard VAT reported as 15% (taxratesbycountry citing PwC). No rule for foreign digital sellers found.
- [V-sec] Jun 2026: a government commission was authorised to talk to digital platforms about "contribution mechanisms"; no rate or law yet (We Are Tech Africa).
- [K] EG is a CEMAC member. Stripe doesn't support sellers there; card use is limited.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Basic privacy notice in Spanish, consent, deletion route; parent consent (or block) under 18.
- Low enforcement risk today; watch for a platform tax.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Possible tax or levy on digital platforms (talks started Jun 2026): no date.
