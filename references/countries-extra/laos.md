# Laos (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for privacy; Med if you sell to Lao consumers (10% VAT on digital services).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: the official law text is only in Lao; no MTC or Tax Department page was read. Lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law on Electronic Data Protection No. 25/NA (12 May 2017), plus 2018 implementing instructions. Covers electronic data only. Regulator: Ministry of Technology and Communications (MTC). (Tilleke & Gibbins via Multilaw, Dig.watch, DataGuidance)
- [V-sec] "General" data can be used without authorisation if the source is stated; "specific" (sensitive) data needs the person's authorisation. (Tilleke & Gibbins)
- [V-sec] Transfer abroad: needs consent; sources disagree whether that is the person's consent (Tilleke) or the data administrator's (Dig.watch). Must not harm national interests.
- [V-sec] Breach: notify LaoCERT (Art. 26); no duty to tell affected users. Fines reported at LAK 5-50 million. (Dig.watch, CaseGuard)
- [V-sec] Foreign reach: no clear extraterritorial clause. (ConsentStack, Multilaw)
- [V-sec] Law on Cybersecurity published in the Official Gazette 20 Mar 2026: updates the definition of personal data, requires immediate incident reporting, annual reports to MTC each January, and registration with MTC for entities that provide online services. No clause aimed directly at offshore providers. (Asia IP Law)
- [K] Age of majority 18; no children's-data age found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is restricted.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Ministry of Finance instruction 0558/MOF (Feb 2024), from 1 Aug 2024: non-resident sellers of digital goods/services (streaming, games, apps, ads...) must register and pay 10% VAT via the DTax system. If a platform sells for you, the platform collects. (ASEAN Briefing, VATCalc, Anrok)
- [V-sec] Threshold disputed: VATCalc cites LAK 400 million a year; Anrok/PayPro say none for non-residents. (VATCalc, Anrok)
- [V-sec] E-commerce Decree 296/GOV (2021) requires sellers to notify the Ministry of Industry and Commerce, but applies to operators resident or registered in Laos. (Tilleke & Gibbins)
- [K] Law on Consumer Protection (2010). No online cooling-off rule found.
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law. The government agreed in principle to a draft National AI Strategy 2026-2035 (Sep 2026), based on a UNESCO ethics readiness assessment. (The Star / Vientiane Times, KPL)
- [V-sec] Decree No. 327 (2014) on internet information bans "false information" against the Party and government; people have been jailed over Facebook posts. Moderate user content. (RFA, Amnesty, Columbia GFoE)

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Selling digital goods to Lao consumers: 10% VAT, or use a Merchant of Record.
- Get consent before moving data abroad (your hosting probably is abroad); say so in your privacy notice.
- Have a breach plan (LaoCERT) if you are big in Laos.
- Moderate user posts about the government if you have Lao users.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Cybersecurity Law implementing regulations (technical standards, penalties) still to come. (Asia IP Law)
- [V-sec] National AI Strategy 2026-2035 going through government. (The Star)

## Unverified
