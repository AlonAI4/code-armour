# Zimbabwe (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: licensing, local representative, 24h breach notice, and conflicting VAT numbers.
Note: no regulator site fetched (potraz.gov.zw, zimra.co.zw not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Cyber and Data Protection Act No. 5 of 2021, effective March 2022; regulator POTRAZ. Applies to anyone handling data of people in Zimbabwe; foreign entities are expected to appoint a local representative. (Michalsons, Clym/consentstack)
- [V-sec] Data controllers must be licensed; Statutory Instrument 155 of 2024 sets fee tiers by number of data subjects (e.g. USD 500 for 100,001-500,000; USD 2,500 for 500,000+); POTRAZ decides within 14 days. Lower-tier fees not found. (DLA Piper Africa, Afriwise)
- [V-sec] Breach: tell POTRAZ within 24 hours; tell affected people within 72 hours if high risk. (DLA Piper Africa)
- [V-sec] Children: parental/guardian consent beforehand; regular impact assessments. (DLA Piper Africa)
- [K] Whether tiny foreign apps must license is unclear.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT: one source says 15.5% from 1 Jan 2026 (Finance Act 2025), others still say 15%. Non-resident digital suppliers: threshold reported as USD 25,000/yr in one source and ZWG 1 million in another; a local representative and fiscalisation are mentioned. CONFLICT, unresolved. (Payproglobal, vatcalc/lookuptax)
- [K] Consumer Contracts Act. Stripe not available for Zimbabwean sellers; EcoCash and USD cash are common.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] The same Act has cybercrime offences (the "cyber" half). (Michalsons)
- [K] No AI law; MISA criticises SI 155 of 2024 as heavy for small actors.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy policy, consent, parent consent for kids, deletion route, 24h breach plan.
- If you have meaningful Zimbabwean users, check POTRAZ licensing and local representative; expect friction.
- Sales: stay low or use a Merchant of Record; do not guess the VAT threshold.
- Moderate chat; report button.
