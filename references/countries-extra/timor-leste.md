# Timor-Leste (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low (no data-protection law in force; no VAT yet).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tracker, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data-protection law in force; no data-protection authority (UNGOVR tracker May 2026; OneTrust DataGuidance/VdA Aug 2025).
- [V-sec] Constitution Art. 38: right to access, correct and know the purpose of personal data; consent needed for some data types.
- [V-sec] Ombudsman (PDHJ) on 15 Jan 2026 urged urgent drafting and passing of a data-protection law, so none was passed then.
- [V-sec] Decree-Law No. 12/2024: e-commerce and e-signatures regime, supervised by TIC Timor. Not a data-protection law.
- [V-sec] Cybercrime bill in draft since ~2021, not enacted as of early 2026.

## Kids and games
- [K] No data-consent age or game rules found. Civil majority commonly given as 17 [unconfirmed]; use parental consent under 18 as a safe default.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No VAT yet. Draft VAT law under public consultation (seminar 18 Sep 2026); government aims for VAT in 2027 with a new tax authority. Whether it covers foreign digital services is unknown (VATCalc).
- [K] Decree-Law 12/2024 may require seller information for e-commerce; details not confirmed. Use a Merchant of Record if selling.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No local privacy law yet: follow your home law and good practice.
- Watch for VAT in 2027 if you sell to Timorese users.
- Watch for a data-protection law (called for in 2026).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] VAT targeted for 2027 (draft law consulted Sep 2026).
- [V-sec] Data-protection law and cybercrime law in preparation (no dates).
