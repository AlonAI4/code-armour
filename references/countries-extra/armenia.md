# Armenia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app. Low-Med if you sell direct (VAT from sale one).
Note: no regulator site (moj.am, src.am) was read; lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law on Protection of Personal Data, in force since 2015 (Law ZR-49), amended later; one source says a revised law (ZR-255, Dec 2022) applied from 1 Jun 2024. Treat the 2015 law plus amendments as the base; GDPR-inspired. Regulator: Personal Data Protection Agency (Ministry of Justice). (Mondaq, Chambers)
- [V-sec] Consent must be free, specific, informed and unambiguous. The Agency published a guide on children's data. (armenian-lawyer.com, moj.am)
- [K] Reach over foreign controllers, breach deadline, children's consent age: not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Law on Regulation of Gaming Activities in force 1 Jan 2025 (gambling geo-blocking). From 8 Feb 2027, telecoms will block unlicensed gambling sites. Loot boxes not addressed in sources, so keep items non-cash-out. (Chambers, Arka)

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident B2C e-services: 20% VAT from the first sale, no threshold (since 1 Jan 2022); simplified e-VAT registration with the State Revenue Committee, quarterly filing. (vatcalc, Kintsugi)
- [V-sec] 14-day cancellation right for remote purchases in force from 1 Jul 2026 (EU CEPA alignment). Digital-content exceptions not confirmed. (Arka)
- [K] Stripe does not onboard Armenian businesses (some work-arounds via Stripe Atlas); merchant of record can sell to buyers there.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. No platform registration rule found.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy and consent; delete on request; extra care with kids' data.
- Selling direct: VAT from sale one; or use a merchant of record. Show the 14-day cancellation (or the digital-content waiver) at checkout.
- No cash-out of in-game currency.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] 8 Feb 2027: blocking of unlicensed gambling sites. A National Strategy on Personal Data Protection 2026-2028 and amendments to the data law are in preparation (no date).
