# Fiji (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low. No data protection law in force. The real risk is the Online Safety Act if your app hosts harmful posts about people in Fiji.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: paclii.org returned 403 (Cloudflare challenge) and laws.gov.fj returned 404, so no Fijian law text was read directly.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general personal data protection law, no data protection authority, no breach-notice, transfer or cookie rules (DLA Piper, updated 19 Mar 2026; DataGuidance).
- [V-sec] Main protection: right to privacy in s.24 of the 2013 Constitution. Sector laws (Banking Act, Cybercrime Act 2021 and others) punish some disclosures but don't regulate apps in general (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Cabinet endorsed a National Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy; government says a law is "being developed", possibly with Council of Europe help, and Fiji plans to join Convention 108+ (FBC News, Sep 2026; Fijivillage). No bill tabled found.
- [K] No children's data age, no cookie rule.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Social Media Age Deferment Taskforce (first met Feb 2026) is working on a bill to set a minimum age of 16 for social media; ministers said they'd table it "later this year" (2026). Not law yet (Fiji Times, Fiji Sun).
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law. Gambling is licensed; keep prizes with no cash value.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT cut from 15% to 12.5% from 1 Aug 2025 (Ministry of Finance budget guide; FedEx notice; Islands Business).
- [V-sec] Digital services by non-residents: vatcalc (Jan 2022) says Fiji proposed VAT on remote digital services by non-resident suppliers and marketplaces (threshold FJD 300,000), date "TBC". Another vendor gives FJD 100,000. Neither confirmed on FRCS. Local business buyers self-account (reverse charge).
- [V-sec] Consumer law: Fijian Competition and Consumer Commission Act 2010 bans unfair and misleading practices; the Consumer Council has used s.88 against an online seller that took payment and didn't deliver (consumersfiji.org). A new Competition and Consumer Commission Bill with stronger online-shopping rights is in consultation (Mai TV).
- [K] No general cooling-off right for online digital purchases.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Online Safety Act 2018 (in force): s.24 makes it a crime to post an electronic communication meant to cause, and that does cause, "harm" (serious emotional distress). Up to FJD 20,000 and/or 5 years for a person; up to FJD 100,000 for a company, and directors up to FJD 50,000 / 7 years. Modelled on NZ's Harmful Digital Communications Act (Fiji High Court 2026 sentencing via search snippet; Fiji Times; Dialogue Fiji).
- [V-sec] The Online Safety Commission (set up by s.6) handles complaints but said in 2025 it cannot order platforms to take content down. A review proposes direct removal orders and duties for service providers; consultations Aug 2026 (FBC News, Fijivillage).
- [K] No AI law. Fiji Times reported "plans to protect AI users"; not read.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No Fiji privacy law to comply with yet: follow your home rules and GDPR-style basics.
- If users can post about others, have a report/remove button and act fast; Fiji prosecutes harmful posts.
- If you sell to Fiji, a Merchant of Record handles any VAT question.
- Watch for the under-16 social media bill if you run a social app.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Under-16 social media bill: government aims to table it in Parliament by end of 2026.
- [V-sec] Online Safety Act amendments (takedown orders, stronger penalties): expected "by end of this year" (2026); no date.
- [V-sec] Data protection law: being drafted; no date.
