# Burundi (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (brand-new privacy law, authority still being set up; details not yet public in English).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] First general data protection law: adopted unanimously by the National Assembly 15 Jan 2026, then the Senate (WeAreTech Africa, Osiris, dataprotection.africa). Two trackers list it as Loi n° 1/03 du 10 mars 2026 portant protection des données à caractère personnel (MoFo privacy library, ConsentStack).
- [V-sec] Creates an independent administrative authority to supervise it; the telecom regulator ARCT handles telecom aspects meanwhile. Sanctions plus Penal Code / ICT-crime law still apply.
- Unverified: reach to foreign developers, children's age, breach deadline, transfer and localisation rules, prior declaration to the authority. French-law (ECOWAS/CEMAC-style) models usually require a prior declaration, consent, and authority approval for transfers: [K].
- [V-sec] Before this: a 2018 decree on data collection/security and the 2024 Electronic Communications Code (confidentiality of communications) (digitalbusiness.africa).

## Kids and games
- [K] Age of majority 18. No game, loot-box or online child-safety law found.

## Selling
- [K] No consumer-protection rule for online sales found (search turned up nothing Burundi-specific).
- [V-sec] VAT: an OBR leaflet (2016) says a non-established supplier with an approved tax representative (VAT law art. 33) must show that representative on invoices. No specific rule found on foreign digital services to consumers (OBR, seen as search snippet).
- [V-sec] Local VAT threshold BIF 25 million (from FY2025, charging from 1 July 2026) (afrotools blog; check with OBR). OBR's table of 2026-27 finance-law changes exists but could not be read.
- Practical: Merchant of Record; Stripe does not support Burundi [K].

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. Strict online-speech climate; 2022 law on ICT offences. Moderate user content.

## Regional
- [K] EAC member; the EAC has no binding data-protection or digital-VAT rule.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Low risk today; follow your general privacy policy.
- Parent consent for under-18s is the safe default.
- Merchant of Record for any paid sales.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data protection authority under Loi 1/03 of 2026 still to be set up; implementing texts expected.

## Unverified
- Official text and Journal officiel entry of Loi 1/03; entry-into-force date (10 Mar 2026 may just be the signing date); every substantive rule of the law; 2026-27 finance-law VAT changes; consumer law; cookies.
