## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 81 of 26 Mar 2019, in force 29 Mar 2021, regulations in Executive Decree 285 (28 May 2021). Regulator ANTAI. Fines USD 1,000-10,000 (Panama judiciary / RSM / consentstack).
- [V-sec] Art. 5: applies to databases located in Panama that hold data of nationals or foreigners, or when the processor is domiciled in Panama (Kennedys, consentstack). So a foreign app with servers abroad is mostly outside it.
- [V-sec] Requires secure-transfer protocols and protection of data subjects' rights (ARCO-type rights).
- [K] Consent required; children: legal-guardian consent; breach notice to ANTAI and people "without delay". Details not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Law 527 (published 28 May, year not confirmed) tightens responsible-gaming rules for online gambling operators, with biometric age checks for under-18s (focusgn). That is for gambling, not ordinary games.
- [K] No loot-box or game age-rating law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No regime currently makes non-resident digital sellers register for ITBMS (payproglobal/Kintsugi-based). [V-sec] A bill (Proyecto 30-26) for 7% ITBMS on digital platforms, with electronic invoicing, was due at Cabinet on 20 Aug 2026 and still needs the National Assembly; minister estimated 60-90 days (La Estrella). Use test: bank location, billing address, IP, SIM country. No threshold or foreign/domestic split stated.
- [K] Consumer law 45/2007 applies. Stripe does not generally support Panamanian businesses [K].

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] AI law under discussion in the National Assembly; national AI strategy with Georgia Tech expected Jun-Jul 2026 (La Estrella, Panama America). Not law.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Spanish privacy policy, consent and deletion path; low enforcement risk for foreign-hosted apps.
- Watch the 7% ITBMS bill; sell via Merchant of Record/app stores so they handle it.
- No gambling-like features.
