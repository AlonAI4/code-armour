# Mauritania (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med on paper (data law reaches foreign apps; 16% VAT on foreign digital services adopted 2026), Low in practice for a tiny foreign app.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. 2017-020 of 22 July 2017 on personal data protection (Journal Officiel no. 1400, 15 Nov 2017). It created the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère personnel (APD), art. 64 (consentstack, offlist, DataGuidance).
- [V-sec] The APD was set up in 2022 (Decree 2022-013; internal rules Oct 2023) and is active: MoU with Algeria's ANPP (Oct 2024) and with Morocco's CNDP (Jan 2026). Data Protection Africa (May 2022) still said the law was not in effect; that is out of date.
- [V-sec] Reach: applies to foreign entities, except pure transit of data (Data Protection Africa).
- [V-sec] Consent is the main basis, with exceptions. Rights: access, objection, correction and deletion; reply within one month (arts. 53-63).
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad: free to countries the APD lists as adequate; otherwise prior notice to the APD, which can set conditions (Data Protection Africa). No localisation rule found.
- [V-sec] No breach-notification duty in the law (Data Protection Africa).
- [V-sec] Criminal and administrative penalties.
- [K] Like other francophone laws based on the ECOWAS/French model, it likely requires a prior declaration to the APD before processing; Data Protection Africa says there is no general register. Not confirmed either way.
- [K] Children: no data-consent age found; age of majority 18.
- [K] Cookies: no cookie rule found.
- [K] Mauritania is not an ECOWAS member (left in 2000), so the ECOWAS data-protection Act does not apply.

## Kids and games
- [K] Child = under 18. Gambling is banned (Islamic law), so avoid cash-value loot boxes or betting features.

## Selling
- [V-sec] 2026 Amending Finance Act (adopted 29 Jul 2026, promulgated about 10 Aug 2026): new art. 210(q) puts "digital services provided by electronic means" (SaaS, cloud, streaming, online ads, AI services) in the VAT scope. Non-resident vendors and platforms must register from the first taxable B2C sale; B2B stays with the local customer/fiscal representative (KPMG, 20 Aug 2026).
- [V-sec] Rate 16% (lookuptax, Kintsugi). Simplified online registration/filing to be set by a Finance Minister order (arrêté), not yet seen.
- [K] No online consumer refund / cooling-off rule found. UNCTAD lists Mauritania as having e-transactions and cybercrime laws (UNCTAD readiness report, content not read).
- Practical: Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] National AI Strategy 2025-2029 (Ministry of Digital Transformation PDF, July 2024; seen in search results only). Strategy only, no AI law.
- [V-sec] Law 2016-006 (Information Society orientation law): sector rules must respect public order, good morals and Islamic values.
- [V-sec] Strict speech laws: a 2020 law on "manipulation of information" carries heavy fines and prison (Human Rights Watch). Moderate user content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy policy and data-subject rights (reply within a month).
- Note your host location; transfers outside APD-listed countries technically need notice to the APD.
- Paid apps: Merchant of Record for the new 16% VAT.
- No gambling-like features; moderate user posts.
