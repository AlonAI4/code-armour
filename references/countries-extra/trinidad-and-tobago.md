# Trinidad and Tobago (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2011 is only partly in force: Part I and parts of Part II proclaimed Jan 2012 (Legal Notice 2 of 2012), s.42(a)-(b) in Aug 2021 (LN 220 of 2021). The private-sector rules and penalties are not proclaimed (DataGuidance, search snippets).
- [V-sec] No Information Commissioner appointed; the job was still being advertised in Sep 2025. No announcement on proclaiming the rest (UWI resource, Feb 2025).
- [K] So today there's no enforceable consent, breach-notice, registration or transfer rule for private apps. The Act's general privacy principles (Part II) are a good model anyway.
- [V-sec] Electronic Transactions Act 2011, s.58: unsolicited commercial e-messages to consumers in T&T must have an easy opt-out; breaking this is an offence.

## Kids and games
- [K] Children Act 2012: child = under 18. No online data-consent age (a 2019 consultation discussed protections for minors).
- [V-sec] Cybercrime Bill (2017, joint select committee report 2019) covers child abuse images and more; not passed as far as found.
- [K] No loot-box law; gambling is licensed.

## Selling
- [V-reg] VAT 12.5%; registration required at TT$500,000 sales in 12 months (Ministry of Finance page; undated).
- [V-sec] No special VAT rules for foreign digital services; the normal threshold applies to non-residents too, and a VAT representative can be required (Grant Thornton). Budget 2025-26 (13 Oct 2025) plans to review VAT and maybe replace it with a simple sales tax (IBFD, KPMG).
- [V-sec] Electronic Transactions Act s.55-57: online sellers must show contact details, payment terms, refund and cancellation policy; if not, the consumer can cancel within 30 days.
- [K] Stripe doesn't support T&T businesses; foreign currency is scarce, so local cards can fail abroad. Use app stores or a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law; no takedown deadlines.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Show your contact info, prices, and refund/cancel policy clearly before checkout (30-day cancel right if you don't).
- Unsubscribe link in every marketing email.
- Merchant of Record for paid sales; below TT$500,000 no VAT registration.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Possible switch from VAT to a sales tax (announced Oct 2025, no date). FY2027 budget expected Oct 2026.
- [K] Full proclamation of the Data Protection Act or a new GDPR-style bill (no date).
