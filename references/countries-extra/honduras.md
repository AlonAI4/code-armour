# Honduras (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: congresonacional.hn, sar.gob.hn and iaip.gob.hn were not opened; mostly news and search snippets.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law. The IAIP (transparency institute) has proposed one since 2015; a GDPR-style draft was presented to Congress in Sep 2024 and was still waiting for a committee report in Aug 2026 (El Heraldo, La Tribuna 10 Aug 2026, UNITEC).
- [K] Constitution art. 182 gives habeas data (right to see and correct your data). The Transparency and Access to Public Information Law covers public bodies, not private apps.
- [K] No breach notice duty, registration, transfer rule or cookie rule for private apps.

## Kids and games
- [K] Childhood and Adolescence Code: child = under 18 (niño under 12, adolescente 12-17). No data-consent age.
- [V-sec] A bill would extend the school bullying law to AI cyberbullying and digital identity theft; still a bill (2026 news).
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law.

## Selling
- [V-sec] ISV (sales tax) 15%. A tax vendor says foreign sellers are "technically" liable to register and charge 15% on digital services to individuals, but no SAR rule or collection system for non-residents was found (PayPro, VATCalc). SAR proposed taxing Netflix/Spotify-type platforms in 2020; not confirmed as enacted.
- [V-sec] The Tax Justice Law (Ley de Justicia Tributaria, in Congress since May 2023) was stuck in third debate in Jul 2025 and was left out of 2026 revenue plans (El Heraldo).
- [K] Consumer Protection Law (2008, regulation 2009) applies to local sales; no online cooling-off period confirmed.
- [K] Stripe doesn't support Honduran businesses. Use app stores or a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law. Conatel announced an AI-risk cybersecurity project with the ITU (Jul 2026); not a law (Marcasur).
- [V-sec] No cybercrime law yet; the Supreme Court approved joining the Budapest Convention in Jun 2026 (TuNota).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Short privacy policy, minimal data, delete on request. Nothing to register or file.
- Sell through app stores or a Merchant of Record.
- Watch the data protection bill and the tax bill.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data protection bill (IAIP draft) pending in Congress, no date. Budapest Convention accession under way (2026).

## Unverified
- Whether SAR really expects foreign digital sellers to register for ISV.
- Online refund rules under the consumer law.
- Exact article numbers of the constitution and children's code.
