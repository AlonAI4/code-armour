# Haiti (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (no data protection statute or regulator; weak enforcement).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official law text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tracker, [K] memory. No official Haitian text (Le Moniteur, DGI) could be read.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] **No general data protection law and no regulator.** The only rule is an *Arrêté* (ministerial order) on personal data protection, Le Moniteur No. 87, 15 May 2018 (DLA Piper, updated Jan 2025; LACDIA privacy notice).
- [V-sec] The Arrêté requires prior consent, secure storage, restricted access, and access/rectification rights; data shared with third parties must stay accessible and correctable by the person. No registration, DPO, breach-notice or cookie rule (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] 2020 Penal Code has data offences (art. 437: unauthorised processing, 1-3 years' jail; arts. 587-593 computer crimes) but its entry into force has been postponed indefinitely (DLA Piper, offlist.me).
- [V-sec] Marketing: get consent (Arrêté) even though the 2020 Code only requires opt-out.

## Kids and games
- [K] No children's data age, loot-box or game-time law. Age of majority 18.

## Selling
- [V-sec] 10% TCA/TVA (turnover tax) on local sales of goods and services and on imports (trade.gov, taxatlas). No rule found for foreign digital-service sellers.
- [V-sec] No consumer protection or e-commerce law found.
- [K] Stripe, Paddle and Lemon Squeezy do not support Haiti-based businesses; card payments from Haiti can fail. Haiti is not under broad sanctions, but some individuals (gang leaders) are sanctioned: payment screening may block names [K].

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law or policy found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Ask for consent before collecting data and let people see and correct it (that is the whole Arrêté in practice).
- No VAT registration rule for foreign sellers found.
- Expect payment and connectivity issues; keep the app light.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] 2020 Penal Code entry into force: postponed, no date.
