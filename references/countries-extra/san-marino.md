# San Marino (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low in practice (about 34,000 people). Med on paper: a near-copy of the GDPR that reaches foreign apps, with EUR 10M fines.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

San Marino is surrounded by **Italy** but is not in the EU or EEA. It uses the euro and is negotiating an EU association agreement. Its privacy law copies the GDPR almost word for word.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Law No. 171 of 21 Dec 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to personal data, in force 5 Jan 2019. Amended by Laws 154/2019, 223/2020, 207/2021 and Delegated Decree 138/2021 (official consolidated English text, consigliograndeegenerale.sm).
- [V-reg] Reach (Art. 3(2)): it applies to controllers and processors **not established** in San Marino that offer goods or services to people there (paid or free) or monitor their behaviour there. Same as GDPR Art. 3(2). No representative duty was found in the text.
- [V-reg] Children (Art. 7): consent alone from **16**. Under 16, a parent must give or authorise consent, and you must make reasonable efforts to check.
- [V-reg] Breach (Art. 34): tell the Data Protection Authority (Garante) within 72 hours; tell users if high risk (Art. 35).
- [V-reg] Cookies (Art. 111): storing or reading info on a user's device needs consent after informing them, except where strictly necessary. Same as EU ePrivacy.
- [V-reg] Transfers (Art. 46): free to EU member states and to countries the EU Commission finds adequate; otherwise use safeguards (Art. 47) or derogations (Art. 50).
- [V-sec] Fines (Art. 72): up to EUR 5M / 2% or EUR 10M / 4% of turnover (Cybersecurity360).
- [V-sec] EU adequacy: none found. The law was drafted to win one; no Commission decision located.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box law found. Gambling is state-licensed.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No EU-style VAT. San Marino uses a single-stage import tax ("monofase", about 17%). Distance sales of **goods** to private buyers face the import tax above EUR 28,000 a year, with a fiscal representative. No rule found that taxes foreign **digital services** sold to consumers (fiscomania, commenda).
- [V-sec] A new general consumption tax (IGC) is reportedly being phased in to align with VAT. Date and scope not confirmed.
- [K] Consumer law: San Marino has a consumer code, but its withdrawal period for online sales was not checked.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. The EU association agreement may later bring EU single-market rules.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- The GDPR checklist in `references/legal.md` covers it: same consent, cookie, 72-hour and under-16 rules.
- Digital-services tax: none found today. Recheck if the IGC or the EU association agreement takes effect.
- If you're in the EU and store data in San Marino, use SCCs (no adequacy found).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] EU association agreement signed at technical level, awaiting ratification by the EU and member states.
- [V-sec] IGC consumption tax phase-in (no date confirmed).

## Unverified
- IGC dates and whether it covers foreign digital services; consumer withdrawal period; adequacy status in 2026; whether Art. 72 fines changed in later amendments.
