## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law on Personal Data Protection No. 3144/2023 (adopted 14 Jun 2023), applied gradually from 1 Mar 2024; GDPR-style, replaces the 2011 law. (Eurofast / Mondaq, Clym)
- [V-sec] Covers foreign businesses processing Georgian residents' data or using Georgian infrastructure. Strong emphasis on children's data and consent for commercial texts. (Eurofast)
- [V-sec] The regulator (Personal Data Protection Service) published a minors' data paper on 30 Jan 2024. (DataGuidance)
- [K] Children's consent age (I believe 16) and breach deadline (72h) not confirmed today; representative duty for foreigners not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident B2C digital services: 18% VAT from the first sale, no threshold (rule since 1 Oct 2021). Register on the Revenue Service's digital-services portal; quarterly returns (Q3 2026: file by 20 Oct, pay by 31 Oct). The general GEL 100,000 threshold does not apply to these sellers. (Anrok, Kintsugi, Bloomberg Tax)
- [K] Consumer law gives 14-day withdrawal for distance sales; digital exceptions unverified.
- [K] Stripe does not onboard Georgian businesses (Georgian developers use workarounds); merchant of record can sell to buyers there. Not verified.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law in Georgia (country) found. No platform registration rule found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- EU-style privacy: policy, consent, deletion/export; extra care with kids' data; parent consent under 16 is the safe default.
- Selling direct means VAT from sale one via the portal; otherwise use a merchant of record.
- Free app: privacy basics only.
