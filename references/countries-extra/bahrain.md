# Bahrain (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: PDPL has foreign reach and VAT has no threshold for non-residents.
Note: no regulator site fetched (pdp.gov.bh, nbr.gov.bh not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Law No. 30 of 2018: published 19 Jul 2018, in force 1 Aug 2019. (Tamimi, Mondaq)
- [V-sec] Reach: also covers people outside Bahrain who process data relating to Bahrain or route processing through it. (Mondaq)
- [V-sec] Children: general guardian consent when the person lacks capacity; no specific age threshold. No rules on automated decisions. (ksandk, DLA summary)
- [V-sec] Ten ministerial resolutions supplement the law (2022 guidelines). (Mondaq, Clyde & Co)
- [K] Breach notice to the authority "without delay"; cross-border needs adequacy or authorisation; fines up to BHD 20,000 and jail. Not confirmed today.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game or social-media age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 10% since 1 Jan 2022. Non-resident digital suppliers must register from the first taxable B2C sale, no turnover threshold. (Avalara, Kintsugi, Payproglobal)
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 35/2012. Stripe/Paddle availability for Bahrain sellers not confirmed.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] National AI Policy launched Jul 2025 plus GCC AI ethics manual: policy only, not a law. (iGA)
- [K] Cybercrime Law 60/2014; strict on religion and the monarchy.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy notice, consent, guardian consent for kids, deletion route.
- Direct digital sales to Bahrainis = register for 10% VAT; a Merchant of Record avoids it.
- Moderate chat; report button.
