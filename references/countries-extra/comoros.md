# Comoros (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (a 2014 law exists on paper; no working authority found).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Loi du 26 juin 2014 portant protection des données à caractère personnel, published by the Ministry of Justice (justice.gouv.km). Covers automated and manual processing. The PDF leaves the law number blank (official PDF seen only as a search snippet; tool could not read it).
- [V-sec] It creates a CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés), but no working complaint route, promulgation record or implementing decree was found; whether it is applicable is unconfirmed (offlist.me review; search summary). The telecom regulator ANRTIC has no data-protection role in the sources.
- [K] French-model law: likely prior declaration to the CNIL, consent, and limits on transfers abroad. Not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [K] Age of majority 18. No game, loot-box or online child-safety law found.

## Selling
- [K] No online consumer-protection rule found.
- [V-sec] Tax: the Comoros has a consumption tax (Taxe sur la Consommation, TC) in the 2023 Code général des impôts, not a VAT; the 2026 Finance Law (Loi n° 25-020/AU, promulgated by decree 26-003/PR of 16 Jan 2026) did not show a digital-services rule in the parts found (official PDFs on justice.gouv.km and finances.gouv.km, seen only as search snippets).
- Practical: Merchant of Record; Stripe does not support the Comoros [K].

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law.

## Regional
- [K] SADC member (since 2017); SADC Model Law on Data Protection (2013) is not binding.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Low risk. Use your general privacy policy and parent consent for under-18s as good practice.
- Merchant of Record for any paid sales.
