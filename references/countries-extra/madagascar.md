# Madagascar (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (French-style data law, regulator only active since Aug 2025, no enforcement on foreign apps known). Paid sales: VAT via a local representative on paper.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. 2014-038 on personal data protection (adopted Dec 2014, promulgated Jan 2015). DLA Piper says it is not fully effective because no implementing decree has been published (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Regulator: Commission Malagasy sur l'Informatique et des Libertés (CMIL), created by Decree 2023-1541; 7 of 9 members appointed 30 Jul 2025 and sworn in Aug 2025; says it is now operational (Newsmada, L'Express de Madagascar).
- [V-sec] Most processing needs a prior declaration to the CMIL; appointing a Madagascar-resident DPO removes the declaration duty (not authorisations) (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad only to countries with sufficient protection, case by case, or with consent/contract necessity; onward transfers need CMIL authorisation (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] No breach-notice duty; general security duty (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] CMIL fines up to 5% of prior-year turnover; criminal: 6 months-2 years for unlawful processing (art. 62) (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Madagascar adopted ratification of the AU Malabo Convention (Law 2024-004, June 2024) (DLA Piper).
- [K] No specific children's-consent age; a minor is under 18. No cookie rule found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is licensed; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 20% applies to digital services by foreign providers (ads, SaaS, hosting, streaming, gaming, e-learning...). Foreign providers must appoint a local representative who collects and pays; no threshold; monthly returns by the 15th. Without a representative, a Malagasy business customer self-accounts (VATCalc, PwC Tax Summaries, Quaderno).
- [K] Consumer law (Loi 2015-014) and e-commerce law (Loi 2014-025) exist; online cooling-off rules not confirmed.
- Practical: a Merchant of Record handles this.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Follow a GDPR-style baseline (the law is modelled on French law).
- Parent consent for minors is a safe default (no specific age in law found).
- Paid sales: Merchant of Record.
- The CMIL is new and focused on local banks, telecoms and government; low risk for a tiny foreign app.
