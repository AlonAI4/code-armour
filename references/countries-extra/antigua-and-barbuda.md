# Antigua and Barbuda (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low. An older (2013) data protection law exists but has no clear foreign reach and an unclear regulator; sales tax may cover streaming/digital services.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2013 (No. 10 of 2013), assented 28 Oct 2013, gazetted 7 Nov 2013; treated as in force from gazettal (Offlist, dig.watch, CaseGuard).
- [V-sec] Regulator: Information Commissioner (under the Freedom of Information Act 2004). Whether the office actually operates for data protection is unclear (Offlist, CaseGuard).
- [V-sec] Uses "data user", not controller. Consent is the default basis; explicit consent for sensitive data (CaseGuard, Offlist).
- [V-sec] No express extraterritorial rule for foreign private companies found; no breach-notice duty found (Offlist, Aug 2026).
- [V-sec] Penalties: fines up to XCD 50,000-100,000 and/or prison (ConsentStack, CaseGuard; vendor summaries).
- [K] No children's age for consent and no cookie rule in the 2013 Act. No new bill found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law. Antigua licenses online gambling; don't offer cash-out prizes.

## Selling
- [V-sec] ABST (sales tax) standard rate 17% from 1 Jan 2024 (Sovos, Inland Revenue announcement Dec 2023).
- [V-sec] ABST extended to online streaming services from 1 Jan 2024; foreign providers "likely" need to register (Rivermate; PM's 2024 budget statement via Orbitax). ABST registration applies to residents and non-residents alike (KPMG 2017).
- [V-sec] Registration threshold XCD 300,000/year (PwC 2018-19, Expanship). Whether it applies to foreign digital sellers is unclear.
- [K] Consumer law: general consumer protection; no online cooling-off right found.
- Practical: Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law found; CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap is non-binding (Jun 2026).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Basic privacy policy and consent; little enforcement risk.
- If you sell subscriptions/streaming, use a Merchant of Record (17% ABST may apply).
- Parent consent for young kids as general good practice (no local age rule).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] ABST e-invoicing announced Dec 2025, no timeline (KPMG).

## Unverified

- Whether the Information Commissioner enforces the 2013 Act.
