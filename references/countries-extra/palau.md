# Palau (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for privacy; Low-Med for tax only if you sell a lot (10% PGST on foreign digital sellers above USD 300,000).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law in force (vendor guides; no statute found).
- [V-sec] Cybersecurity Act (Senate Bill 12-9, SD1, HD2) passed the Olbiil Era Kelulau on 20 Jul 2026 and went to the President; no signing reported as of this research (Island Times, 24 Jul 2026). It creates a Bureau of Cybersecurity and breach reporting for government agencies; it does not appear to regulate private apps.
- [K] The Constitution protects privacy in general terms.
- No children's consent age for data, no breach-notice rule for private apps, no localisation rule found.

## Compact of Free Association with the US
- [K] Palau is a sovereign state in a Compact of Free Association with the US and uses the US dollar. US federal laws (COPPA, state privacy laws, FTC Act) do not apply as Palauan domestic law. They still apply to you if you are a US-based developer, as they would anywhere.

## Kids and games
- [K] No kids' online or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Palau Goods and Services Tax (PGST), 10%, since 1 Jan 2023. Non-resident suppliers of remote/electronic services to non-registered people in Palau must register and collect once Palau sales exceed USD 300,000 a year (Sovos; PayPro Global). A small app is far below this.
- [K] No consumer refund law for online sales found.
- Practical: a Merchant of Record handles PGST if you ever get near the threshold.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No general privacy law: follow the general rules in SKILL.md.
- PGST only matters above USD 300,000 of Palau sales.
- Don't assume US law applies "because Palau uses the dollar"; it doesn't, as Palauan law.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Cybersecurity Act awaiting presidential signature (passed 20 Jul 2026). Mostly government-facing.
