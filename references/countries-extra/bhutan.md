# Bhutan (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low (no standalone privacy law; new 5% GST has a high threshold small apps won't reach).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No standalone data-protection law and no data-protection authority (offlist.me review Aug 2026; consentstack). No bill found.
- [V-sec] Information, Communications and Media Act 2018 (in force 8 Jan 2018): privacy chapters (online/offline privacy, data protection), with consent, notice, correction, security, child-data and direct-marketing duties; regulator BICMA. Applies to ICT/media service providers; reach to foreign apps unclear. Section numbers differ across sources.
- [V-sec] National Data Governance Framework (in force 11 Jul 2025) says national privacy regulations are still missing and assigns their drafting to government bodies.
- [K] No localisation or breach-notice rule known for foreign apps.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] BICMA Rules and Regulations on Content 2019 include a section on children's content (contents not confirmed). [K] Age of majority 18; no data-consent age found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] GST at 5% from 1 Jan 2026 (GST Act 2020 as amended 2025) replaced sales tax. Non-resident digital-service suppliers (software, games, streaming, websites...) must register via a local representative once Bhutanese turnover exceeds Nu 5 million/year (voluntary from Nu 2.5 million). B2B: reverse charge (Fonoa, Anrok, Sovos). One source (PayPro) says no threshold; conflicting.
- [K] Nu 5 million is about USD 60,000; a small app won't reach it. Use a Merchant of Record anyway.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. BICMA content rules apply to licensed providers.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No local privacy law to follow beyond good practice; parental consent for under-18s as a safe default.
- GST only matters above Nu 5M of Bhutanese sales; an MoR handles it if you sell.
- Check back: privacy regulations are planned under the 2025 framework.

## Upcoming
