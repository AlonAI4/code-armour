# Marshall Islands (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Personal Data Protection Act 2025 (P.L. 2025-43, 6 MIRC Ch. 4), commencement 7 Oct 2025. It applies only to core Government ministries and agencies, not private businesses or foreign apps. Competent authority: Economic Policy, Planning and Statistics Office (the Act spells it "EPSSO"). Takes effect 12 months after certification (§418), so about Oct 2026 if certified on 7 Oct 2025.
- [V-sec] No general data protection law for the private sector (DataGuidance; SEAP).
- [K] Constitution protects against unreasonable search; Criminal Code 2011 covers eavesdropping and breach of message privacy; financial-sector laws have confidentiality duties.
- No children's consent age for data, no breach-notice rule for private apps, no localisation rule found.

## Compact of Free Association with the US
- [K] The RMI is a sovereign state in a Compact of Free Association with the US and uses the US dollar. US federal laws (COPPA, FTC Act, state privacy laws) do not apply as RMI domestic law. They still apply to you if you are a US-based developer.

## Kids and games
- [K] No kids' online or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Consumption Tax Act 2025 (P.L. 2025-47 per PacLII file name) creates a Marshall Islands Consumption Tax meant to replace import duties (PacLII listing; IMF 2025 report). Rate, start date and whether foreign digital sellers must register: not found.
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Act (20 MIRC Ch. 4): bans unfair/deceptive practices; the government can sue to recover money paid. No general cooling-off/refund right found (PacLII listing).
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record so a future consumption tax on digital sales is handled for you.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No privacy law reaches private apps: follow the general rules in SKILL.md.
- Don't mislead buyers (Consumer Protection Act).
- Watch the new consumption tax for digital-services rules.
