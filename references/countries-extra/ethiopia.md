# Ethiopia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (enforcement capacity is limited), but the privacy law is strict on paper.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, in force 24 Jul 2024. Regulator: Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA) (TechHive Advisory).
- [V-sec] Scope: controllers established in Ethiopia or using equipment there; reach to purely foreign web apps is unclear.
- [V-sec] All controllers/processors must register with the Authority (renew every 2 years).
- [V-sec] Minor = under 16: parent/guardian consent needed.
- [V-sec] Breach notice to Authority and affected people within 72 hours.
- [V-sec] Localisation: data collected must be stored on a server/data centre in Ethiopia; transfers abroad via adequacy, explicit consent or necessity; sensitive data abroad needs prior Authority approval.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game or loot-box law found. Gambling is licensed by the National Lottery; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT Proclamation No. 1341/2024 (2024): remote/digital services by non-residents through electronic platforms are VATable at 15% (EY, Fonoa).
- [V-sec] Registration threshold reported as ETB 2M in 12 months (Kintsugi). Implementing directive was in draft (Oct 2024, KPMG); final status not confirmed.
- Practical: tiny sales are under the threshold; Merchant of Record is the easy path.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] Hate Speech and Disinformation Proclamation 1185/2020: platforms should remove such content (24h expectation). Internet shutdowns happen regionally.
- [K] No AI law; national AI policy (2024) only.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Parent consent (or block) under 16.
- 72-hour breach plan.
- Moderate user content quickly (hate speech).
- Accept that registration/localisation rules exist but are rarely enforced on small foreign apps; revisit if you get many Ethiopian users.

## Upcoming
- [K] Data protection directives from the ECA expected; nothing dated found.

## Unverified
- Final VAT directive for non-residents and its start date; whether the PDP Proclamation reaches foreign-only apps; fines.
