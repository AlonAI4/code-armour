# Angola (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app: old law with large fines on paper; foreign digital VAT with no threshold.
Note: no regulator site fetched (apd.ao not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 22/11 of 17 Jun 2011 on personal data; regulator APD (set up 2016, more active in recent years). Applies to controllers based or operating in Angola, or using equipment subject to Angolan law. (Hunton, PLMJ, DLA Piper Africa)
- [V-sec] Principles: transparency, lawfulness, proportionality, purpose, accuracy, retention limits. Registration/notification with APD; fines reported up to USD 150,000 (USD 7,000-15,000 for not notifying). (consentstack, PLMJ)
- [V-sec] No explicit child-consent rule in the current law; the 2025 draft revision adds parental consent, 72h breach notice, rules for AI processing and portability. Consultation 17 Mar-17 Apr 2025; not enacted as of May 2026. (TechHive, CMS)
- [V-sec] 25 Jun 2026: parliament rejected a separate UNITA data-protection bill about voter data. Not the APD revision. (AllAfrica)

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT (IVA) 14%. Since 1 Oct 2019 non-residents supplying distance/digital services to Angolan consumers must register and charge IVA; no threshold. (Kintsugi, Payproglobal)
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 15/03. Stripe not available for Angolan sellers; local gateways (Multicaixa Express) dominate.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law; Angola's cybercrime and media laws exist; no takedown deadline found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Privacy policy (Portuguese is smart), consent, deletion route; parent consent for kids as best practice.
- Direct sales to Angolans: register for 14% IVA or use a Merchant of Record or app stores.
- Watch the revision of Law 22/11: 72h breach notice and AI rules may come.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Revised data-protection law: draft only, no date.
