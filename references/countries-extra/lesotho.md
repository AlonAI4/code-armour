# Lesotho (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (a privacy law exists, but its regulator has never been set up).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act, 2011, published as Act No. 5 of 2012 and in force from gazettal on 22 Feb 2012 (LesothoLII listing; dataprotection.africa).
- [V-sec] The Data Protection Commission it creates has still not been appointed, so in practice nobody enforces it; even when set up it has no fining power (courts only) (dataprotection.africa, DataGuidance, a Sep 2026 review).
- [V-sec] Reach: controllers domiciled in Lesotho, and non-domiciled controllers that use automated or non-automated means in Lesotho. Based on POPIA-style wording; a foreign app on foreign servers is arguably outside it (search summaries of the Act).
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad (s.52): allowed where the other country's law is substantially similar, or with consent, contract necessity, or binding corporate rules.
- [V-sec] Right to object to direct marketing.
- [K] No child-specific consent age found in the Act; age of majority is 18.
- [K] No breach-notice deadline found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box, game-time or online child-safety law found. Treat under-18s as children.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No general consumer-protection law for online sales found. An EUI database says Lesotho has no framework applying consumer protection to online transactions; a 2013 E-Transactions and E-Commerce Bill (with a cooling-off clause) was drafted but no evidence it passed. The Financial Consumer Protection Act 2022 (in force 3 June 2022) covers financial products only.
- [V-sec] VAT: no rule found that makes foreign digital sellers register. In 2021 the revenue authority said there was no legislation for taxing foreign digital services (The Reporter). VAT registration threshold M2,000,000; e-invoicing (Lekuka) from 2026 applies only to VAT-registered vendors (VATCalc / vendor posts).
- Practical: a Merchant of Record keeps you covered if this changes.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. Computer Crime and Cyber Security Act 2023 exists (content offences); not checked.

## Regional
- [K] SADC member; the SADC Model Law on Data Protection (2013) is a template, not binding. No SADC-wide digital VAT rule.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Low risk. Follow your general privacy policy and good practice (consent for marketing, easy opt-out).
- Treat under-18s carefully even without a specific rule.
- Use a Merchant of Record so a future digital VAT rule is handled for you.

## Upcoming
- None dated. Watch for the Data Protection Commission being appointed and any VAT amendment on digital services.

## Unverified
- Whether a VAT amendment on imported digital services has passed since 2021; children's provisions; breach rules; cookie rules (none found). LesothoLII blocked the tool (403), so the Act text was not read.
