# Albania (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. VAT is the main cost if you sell direct.
Note: the Commissioner's site (idp.al) appeared in results but was not read; lines are secondary unless noted.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 124/2024 on Personal Data Protection: adopted 19 Dec 2024, published 17 Jan 2025 (Off. Gazette 9), in force 31 Jan 2025, with some articles delayed up to two years. GDPR-aligned; replaces Law 9887/2008. (KPMG Albania, PwC Albania)
- [V-sec] Applies to foreign controllers handling people in Albania; local representative required. Breach notice to the Commissioner within 72h. (CMS, Clym)
- [V-sec] Online services aimed at under-16s need parent/guardian consent. (CMS snippet)
- [V-sec] Direct marketing rules aligned with GDPR (opt-in). (Karanovic)

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or loot-box law found. Online gambling is restricted by gambling law; not searched in detail.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident B2C digital services: VAT (TVSH) 20% from the first sale, no threshold (rule since 2015). A local tax / fiscal representative is mandatory and there is no OSS-style simplification. Monthly filing by the 14th. (Grant Thornton, vatcalc, Anrok)
- [K] Consumer protection law has a 14-day withdrawal for distance contracts; digital-content exceptions unverified.
- [K] Stripe does not onboard Albanian businesses; merchant of record needed to sell from there. Not verified.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No standalone AI law; AI is covered by data, consumer and cybersecurity laws. (CMS AI scanner)

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- EU-style privacy: policy, consent, deletion, 72h breach plan; parent consent under 16.
- Selling directly means 20% VAT from sale one plus a fiscal rep: use a merchant of record or app stores.
- A free app with a few Albanian users is low enforcement risk; the representative duty is mainly a paper risk.

## Upcoming
