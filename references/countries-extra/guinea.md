# Guinea (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med on paper (prior declaration + authorisation for transfers abroad; new 2026 levy on foreign digital services), Low in practice for a tiny foreign app (data authority not clearly working; small providers under the levy threshold).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: this is Guinea (Conakry), not Guinea-Bissau or Equatorial Guinea. The official journal (journal-officiel.sgg.gov.gn) PDF tried was a scan with no text; no regulator or tax site read.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. L/2016/037/AN of 28 Jul 2016 on cybersecurity and personal data protection. It provides for a data protection authority "established by regulatory means" (art. 47), not effective as of the DLA Piper review (2021). (DLA Piper, dataprotection.africa)
- [V-sec] Prior declaration (receipt before processing) or authorisation; authorisation for genetic, medical, biometric data and for transfers to third countries. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Transfers: only to countries with higher or equal protection, plus prior authorisation; dataprotection.africa says the restriction applies to transfers outside ECOWAS. (DLA Piper, dataprotection.africa)
- [V-sec] Unsolicited direct marketing without prior written consent: 1-5 years' prison and GNF 30M-200M fine. (DLA Piper) So: opt-in for marketing emails/SMS.
- [V-sec] Apr 2025 decree (read on TV 14 Apr 2025) on security of civil-status and ID data; mentions "the authority in charge of personal data protection" referring offenders to the prosecutor. It targets ID registers, not apps; no localisation found. (Guinéenews)
- [V-sec] No breach-notification duty found; no children's age found. (dataprotection.africa)

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time, social-age or loot-box rule found. Majority is 18; get parent consent for young users.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 18% on digital services by foreign suppliers listed from Jan 2016, no threshold (vatcalc global table only; no Guinean text found).
- [V-sec] New 2026 decree: "Redevance de Conformité Numérique" (RCN) on foreign digital services consumed in Guinea (streaming, games, ads, cloud/SaaS, app stores, e-commerce commissions). 3% flat for the first 12 months, later a grid set by the telecom regulator ARPT. Exempt if annual taxable base is under GNF 250M; free educational/cultural services exempt. Representative to ARPT within 90 days; monthly declarations. Location by card address, IP, phone or billing address. Collection starts after a 6-month transition. (Guinéenews 22 May 2026, Osiris)
- [V-sec] Stripe Tax lists Guinea (tax calculation only). (Condia)
- [K] Stripe does not onboard sellers in Guinea; mobile money (Orange Money, MTN) dominates.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] National AI roadmap 2026-2035 validated Dec 2025 (with UNDP); no AI law. Guinea is in the 2026 shared non-binding francophone AI ethics guidelines. (Osiris, WeAreTech)
- [K] The transitional government has restricted social media and internet access at times; moderate political content.

## Regional blocs and sanctions
- [V-sec] ECOWAS lifted its remaining economic sanctions on Guinea in Feb 2024 (with Niger, Mali, Burkina Faso). (Blueprint, ICIR)
- [K] Guinea did not join the AES and did not announce an ECOWAS exit; its ECOWAS membership status after the 2021 coup suspension was not checked. Not in UEMOA (own currency, the Guinean franc).
- [K] No country-wide US/EU/UN sanctions; not in `sanctioned-places.md`.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- French privacy policy, consent, opt-in for marketing messages; parent consent for kids.
- Transfers abroad technically need authorisation, but the authority isn't clearly running; keep data minimal.
- Selling to people in Guinea: use a Merchant of Record or app stores. A tiny app is under the RCN levy threshold (GNF 250M a year).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] RCN levy collection starts after a 6-month transition from the decree's signing (decree reported May 2026, so about late 2026); 3% rate for 12 months, then an ARPT grid. Add to upcoming.md when merged. (Guinéenews)
