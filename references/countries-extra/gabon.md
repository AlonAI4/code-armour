# Gabon (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: an active data authority (inspections of foreign airlines), parent consent under 18, a 2026 ordinance on AI labelling and deepfakes, and new VAT on foreign digital sales.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory. Law text read from the AFAPDP copy of Law 025/2023; regulator site not read.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Law No. 025/2023 of 12 Jul 2023 amending Law No. 001/2011 on personal data protection. Regulator: APDPVP (Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles et de la Vie Privée).
- [V-reg] Applies to controllers in Gabon, and to controllers established or not in Gabon that use means of processing in Gabon; they must name a representative in Gabon. Art. 172: APDPVP can act on processing carried out wholly or partly in Gabon "y compris lorsque le responsable du traitement est établi sur le territoire d'un autre Etat".
- [V-reg] Breaches likely to seriously harm people's rights: notify APDPVP "sans délai excessif" (no fixed hours).
- [V-reg] Fines XAF 1-100 million; first breach capped at XAF 98.4 million; repeat up to XAF 300 million or, for a company, 5% of turnover (capped at XAF 196 million).
- [V-sec] Most processing must be declared to APDPVP; prior authorisation for biometric, genetic and criminal data. Transfers only to countries APDPVP deems adequate, or with express consent / contract exceptions (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] APDPVP is active: inspections of Airtel, Royal Air Maroc, Air France (May 2025), declaration campaigns, a public warning in Jun 2026. No published fine found (Gabonreview, Gabon Media Time).

## Kids and games
- [V-reg] Art. 74: processing a child's data is lawful from age 18; under 18 a parent must expressly authorise it, and the controller must check by any means that the parent really consented. The law also has a chapter on child privacy online (Arts. 188-192).
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Corrective Finance Law (LFR) No. 002/2026, reported promulgated 17 Jul 2026: foreign digital service providers without a presence in Gabon owe VAT (18%) and the special solidarity contribution (CSS); simplified direct registration, no local representative needed. Covers streaming, cloud, software, ad platforms, online games (Infos Gabon, Gabon Media Time).
- [V-sec] Start date not given; press says "by end of 2026". No threshold found: treat as from sale 1.
- [K] No online cooling-off right confirmed. Stripe doesn't support sellers in Gabon; Airtel Money common.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Ordinance No. 0011/PR/2026 of 26 Feb 2026 (published Apr 2026): bans non-consensual deepfakes of identifiable people and fake statements attributed to people; platforms must label AI-generated content with a visible, clear, permanent mark (Art. 53); people can demand removal of AI content about them within 24 hours (Gabon Media Time).
- [V-sec] Osiris reports fines up to XAF 50 million on social networks and press says the text ends online anonymity (real name, address and ID to access platforms). Not confirmed in the text.
- [V-sec] National AI strategy in preparation (workshops Sep 2026) (Gabonreview, Osiris).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Parent consent (or block) under 18; check it is really the parent.
- If users post content or you generate images/voices with AI: label AI output visibly, ban deepfakes of real people, and remove complaints within 24 hours.
- Selling: Merchant of Record or app stores (new 18% VAT + CSS).
- French privacy notice, deletion route, breach plan.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Start of VAT on foreign digital services under LFR 2026: expected by end 2026.
- [V-sec] National AI strategy (horizon 2030): no date.
