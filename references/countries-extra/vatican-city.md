## Privacy
- [V-sec] General Regulation on the Protection of Personal Data, Decree No. DCLVII of the Pontifical Commission for Vatican City State, promulgated and in force 30 Apr 2024, "ad experimentum" for three years (Digital Policy Alert, iubenda, Vatican announcement via search).
- [V-sec] Inspired by the EU GDPR but adapted; principles include legitimate purpose and consent; sensitive data (ethnic origin, political opinions, biometrics) prohibited with exceptions.
- [V-sec] Scope: processing by the Governorate of Vatican City State; Holy See / Roman Curia processing is excluded. Not aimed at foreign apps.
- [K] Vatican City is not in the EU, so the GDPR does not apply there as such; but a visitor in Rome is in Italy (EU) and the GDPR applies to them. Italian law does not govern Vatican territory.

## Selling
- [K] Vatican City is outside the EU VAT area; no VAT on digital services sold to its residents. Sales to people in Italy follow EU/Italian VAT.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] Vatican City State issued AI guidelines (Decree, Jan 2025) for its own bodies; not aimed at foreign apps.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Nothing extra in practice. Treat it like the EU for good practice (GDPR-style policy covers it).
