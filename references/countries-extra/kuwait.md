# Kuwait (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app. No VAT; privacy rules are a telecom-regulator regulation, not a full law.
Note: no regulator site fetched (citra.gov.kw not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] CITRA Data Privacy Protection Regulation, Resolution No. 42 of 2021, effective 4 Apr 2021: user consent, purpose limits, security, parental consent for children's data. (Clym, Tamimi)
- [V-sec] Sources CONFLICT: Decision No. 26 of 2024 reportedly narrowed it to telecom service providers and licensees; another source says it covers websites, apps and cloud providers and was enforced from 1 Jan 2025. Unresolved. (Chambers, Telecom Review)
- [K] No general data protection law; breach deadline, local representative, and localisation not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Parental consent for children's data (CITRA regulation, above). [K] No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No VAT or general sales tax in force in 2026. The 2026-2030 fiscal plan leaves VAT out; none expected before 2028. GCC framework rate would be 5%. (VATupdate, Kintsugi)
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 39/2014 exists. Stripe is not available to Kuwait-based sellers; Merchant of Record covers buyers there.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Cybercrime Law No. 63 of 2015, in force 12 Jan 2016; articles on online speech criticised as broad. (Wikipedia, Article 19)
- [K] No AI law. Strict rules on religion, the Emir and public morals in online content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Short privacy policy with consent; parent consent for kids.
- Moderate chat for insults to the Emir, religion and similar; keep a report button.
- Nothing to collect on tax today; re-check if Kuwait adopts VAT.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] VAT: not planned before 2028.
