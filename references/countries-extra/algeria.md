# Algeria (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: foreign-digital VAT is on paper at 19% with a fiscal representative, but status is contested.
Note: no regulator site fetched (anpdp.dz, mfdgi.gov.dz not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 18-07 on personal data, effective in full 10 Aug 2023; regulator ANPDP. Applies to foreign companies processing data of people in Algeria. (Cookie-Script, DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Explicit, informed, specific consent; children: consent of the legal representative, or a judge's authorisation. (Cookie-Script)
- [V-sec] 15 Jul ANPDP decision tightens rules on collecting and sharing identifiable images. (Ecofin)
- [K] Prior declaration/authorisation to ANPDP; transfers abroad restricted; breach duties; jail and fines. Not confirmed today.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social-media age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 19% on foreign digital services (streaming, software, e-books, online games) since 2022; non-resident appoints a fiscal representative and registers with the DGI; B2B reverse charge. (vatcalc, Kintsugi)
- [V-sec] 2026 Finance Law adds wording on "non-resident digital economy actors". Penalties up to 200% of unpaid tax are claimed by one source. (Algeria Tech)
- [V-sec] CONFLICT: a vatcalc headline says the 19% digital VAT was suspended after inflation worries. Current status unclear.
- [K] E-commerce Law 18-05 (sites should be .dz and hosted locally). Stripe/PayPal not available; payments via CIB/Edahabia. Not confirmed.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law; content and cybercrime rules are strict; internet shutdowns during exams happen.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy and explicit consent; parent consent for kids; avoid sharing identifiable photos without consent.
- Sales: Merchant of Record or app stores for Algerian buyers; do not register yourself at small scale.
- Moderate chat; report button.
