# Samoa (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low. No general data protection law found; no tax rule for foreign digital sellers confirmed.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: paclii.org returned 403 (Cloudflare challenge); palemene.ws (Parliament) PDF links returned 404. No Samoan law text was read directly.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No standalone data protection law; the Telecommunications Act 2005 is the main privacy rule, and it only covers telecom providers (customer data not disclosed without written consent) (PACER Plus privacy page; The Art of Service). Weak sources, but no source found showing a general law or a bill.
- [V-sec] National Digital Identification Act 2024 protects data in the national digital ID system and requires breach reports to affected people. Only for the government ID system and its "relying parties" (nationalityforall.org).
- [K] No children's data age, no cookie rule, no transfer rule for private apps.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] No social media age law or online-safety bill found; a Dec 2024 Samoa Observer street poll asked whether Samoa needs an Australia-style under-16 ban.
- [K] No loot-box law.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAGST (Value Added Goods and Services Tax) 15% under the VAGST Act 2015; registration threshold about WST 130,000 a year (Expanship; lookuptax).
- [V-sec] The Act has a section on "GST representatives of non-residents" (s.44); one vendor says foreign digital sellers use a representative, another says the Samoan buyer self-assesses. No official guidance found; treat as unclear.
- [K] Consumer protection: Competition and Consumer Act 2016; no online cooling-off right found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. Crimes Act 2013 has some online-harassment offences; Samoa re-criminalised defamation in 2017 and has used it over Facebook posts, so moderate posts about named people.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No Samoan privacy law: follow your home rules and good basics.
- If you sell, use a Merchant of Record; you're far below the VAGST threshold.
- Remove defamatory or abusive posts about named Samoans when reported.
