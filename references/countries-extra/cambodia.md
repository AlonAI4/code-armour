# Cambodia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here. Med if you sell more than about USD 61k/yr to Cambodians (VAT).
Note: only search snippets and summaries were read; no regulator site (tax.gov.kh, mptc.gov.kh) was opened. [V-sec] = seen in law-firm/news text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law is in force. A Law on Personal Data Protection (LPDP) was drafted by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications; draft released 23 Jul 2025, workshops held, not enacted as of mid-Feb 2026 (Hogan Lovells / DLA Piper pages).
- [V-sec] Once enacted it is expected to start after a 2-year transition from promulgation. Not confirmed that it has been promulgated since.
- [K] Today only scattered rules (E-commerce Law 2019, Telecom Law) touch personal data. Children's consent age and breach deadline for the draft: unknown.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] No minors-in-games law found. News: PM Hun Manet announced a ban on online gambling at casinos from Oct 2026 (The Star, 19 Sep 2026). That targets gambling, not ordinary games.
- [K] Loot boxes: no rule found. Keep coins non-cashable.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident digital suppliers (B2C and B2B) must register for VAT within 30 days once Cambodian turnover reaches KHR 250M/yr (about USD 61k) or KHR 60M over 3 consecutive months. Rate 10%, monthly returns, tax paid in riel by the 20th (DFDL, ASEAN Briefing). Registration fee KHR 400,000.
- [V-sec] B2B: supplier files but does not pay; Cambodian business buyer reverse-charges.
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Law and E-commerce Law both enacted 2 Nov 2019 (Tilleke). [K] Details on refunds and seller-info display not checked.
- [K] Stripe is not available to Cambodian-based businesses; use a Merchant of Record (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) or app stores.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law. A Draft National AI Strategy 2025-2030 (strategy, not law) was circulated June 2025 (Rajah & Tann).
- [K] Content: speech and "incitement" offences in the Criminal Code are used against online posts; moderate hate/political content in chat if Cambodia-facing.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Treat Cambodian users like any: short privacy policy, collect little data, honour deletion requests.
- Stay below KHR 250M/yr Cambodian sales, or use a Merchant of Record (it handles VAT).
- Keep coins non-cashable; no gambling features.
- Add a report/remove path for chat content.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] LPDP could be promulgated any time; check status before launch.
- [V-sec] Online gambling ban at casinos from Oct 2026.
