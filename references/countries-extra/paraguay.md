# Paraguay (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here. The new privacy law is not in force yet.
Note: only search snippets read; dnit.gov.py and the new data agency were not opened. [V-sec] = law-firm/news text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 7593/2025 on Personal Data Protection promulgated 27 Nov 2025; enters into force about 24 months later (about Nov 2027). Creates a National Data Protection Agency inside MITIC. Includes lawful bases, security duties, breach notification, impact assessments, international-transfer safeguards, individual rights, and reinforced protection for children and teens (Allende, Olarte Moure, EC IP helpdesk).
- [V-sec] Applies to "public or private, national or foreign" companies processing data without a legal basis (Kiteworks summary). Exact foreign reach, breach deadline and child age: not confirmed.
- [V-sec] Law 7549/25 requires data retention by internet providers to fight child abuse material (DPL News).
- [K] Until 2027 there is only the constitutional habeas data right and sector rules (Law 1682/2001).

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Executive drafting a bill for children in the digital environment (grooming, AI content, device use); Congress bill on grooming; La Nacion 26 May 2026. Not law yet.
- [K] No loot-box or age-rating law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Digital services from non-residents: income is Paraguay-source; a 10% IVA perception is added to the price and a 4.5% effective Non-Resident Income Tax retention is subtracted. Provider without a local representative notifies the tax authority within 10 business days of starting (DNIT Res. 114/22, Ferrere). Collection is largely through payers/card issuers [K].
- [V-sec] Use in Paraguay is judged by IP/SIM country, billing address, bank or card issuer.
- [K] No small-seller threshold found. Consumer law (Law 1334/98) applies; details not checked.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. [V-sec] AI-content risks are named in the children's bill.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Nothing mandatory on privacy today, but build to the 2027 law: policy, consent, deletion, breach plan, extra care with under-18s.
- Selling: use a Merchant of Record or app stores; they deal with the 10% IVA and 4.5% retention.
- Add a report/remove option for chat.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Law 7593 in force about Nov 2027 (needs regulations within 24 months).
- [V-sec] Children-in-digital-environment bill pending.

## Unverified
- Breach deadline, children's consent age and foreign-reach wording in Law 7593.
- Whether the 10% / 4.5% rules are actively enforced on small foreign sellers.
- Consumer-law cooling-off for digital goods.
