# Mozambique (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (no general data law yet; new digital VAT, but its B2C mechanics for foreign sellers are unclear).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law. Privacy comes from the Constitution, the Electronic Transactions Law (Law 3/2017) and the AU Malabo Convention (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Law 3/2017: keep data accurate and secure, name someone responsible, give prior notice of processing to INTIC; email marketing needs express consent (with an existing-customer exception); fines of 30-90 minimum public-sector wages (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] Transfers: no specific restriction in law, but the Constitution bars disclosure to third parties without consent; DLA Piper suggests consent or a court order for export.
- [V-sec] No breach-notice duty (a Cybersecurity Bill, v6 Sept 2023, would add one) (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] A Personal Data Protection Bill is before the Assembleia da República (INTIC, Aug 2026); reportedly approved by the Council of Ministers 3 Mar 2026 (one promotional source). It would cover anyone processing data in Mozambique (AIM, PLMJ).
- [K] No children's-consent age or cookie rule in force.

## Kids and games
- [K] Child = under 18. No loot-box or game-time law found. Gambling is licensed; no cash-out items.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Law 10/2025 (29 Dec 2025), from 1 Jan 2026: digital goods and services explicitly in VAT (16%), whether the supplier is local or foreign. B2B: the Mozambican buyer self-accounts and files a monthly return by the 10th (KPMG, DLA Piper Africa, RSM, PwC).
- [V-sec] B2C: one source says no threshold and a local fiscal representative; others don't describe B2C collection (Regfollower). Treat as "may apply from sale 1".
- [K] Consumer law: Lei 22/2009 (Lei de Defesa do Consumidor). Online cooling-off rules not confirmed.
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Decree 59/2023 (in force Dec 2023): intermediary e-service providers and digital platform operators serving users in Mozambique must register/licence with INTIC, wherever they are based; amended by Decree 44/2025 (Afriwise, AIM, DLA Piper). Unclear if a tiny app counts as a "platform operator".
- [V-sec] Cloud (Decree 70/2025) and data-centre (Decree 71/2025) regulations need INTIC registration/licensing for providers (DLA Piper). Aimed at providers, not app makers.
- [K] No AI law.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Follow a GDPR-style baseline; no Mozambique-specific privacy duties likely to be enforced on you.
- Paid sales: Merchant of Record (new 16% digital VAT).
- If you run a platform with real Mozambican user numbers, check INTIC registration under Decree 59/2023.
- Watch the data protection bill.
