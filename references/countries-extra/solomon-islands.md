# Solomon Islands (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low. No data protection law, no VAT yet.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No data protection law; UNCTAD is supporting drafting (law.ungovr.org tracker, May 2026, low confidence). No bill found.
- [V-sec] Telecommunications Act 2009 bars telecom providers from collecting, using or disclosing user information except as allowed; only covers telecom providers (nationalityforall.org).
- [V-sec] National Cybersecurity Policy launched Aug 2024; policy, not law.
- [K] No children's data age, cookie or transfer rule.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] No online safety or social media age law found. In 2020 Cabinet agreed to "temporarily" block Facebook citing harmful content; unclear if enforced (Global Voices, IFJ).
- [K] No loot-box law.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No VAT/GST today: sales tax and goods tax on local goods and imports (vendor guides, conflicting details).
- [V-reg] Value Added Tax Bill 2025 (No. 8 of 2025) is before Parliament; Bills and Legislation Committee submissions closed 3 Mar 2026 (parliament.gov.sb call for submissions).
- [V-sec] Draft: single 15% rate on taxable supplies, imports and imported services (VATupdate, Jul 2025). IRD plans VAT start in 2028 (VATupdate, Sep 2026; In-Depth Solomons, 1 Sep 2026).
- [K] Consumer Protection Act (Cap 63) is old and covers goods prices and safety; no online refund rule found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. No takedown law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Nothing Solomon Islands-specific to register for or comply with today.
- Follow your home-country rules and good privacy basics.
- If you sell, a Merchant of Record will handle VAT when it starts (planned 2028).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] VAT (15%, incl. imported services): planned for 2028; bill still before Parliament.
- [V-sec] Data protection law: being drafted with UNCTAD; no date.

## Unverified
- Whether the VAT bill has passed; whether it makes foreign digital sellers register.
- Any 2025-2026 online safety bill.
