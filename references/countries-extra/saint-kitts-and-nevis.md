# Saint Kitts and Nevis (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (data protection law passed but not in force; no digital-services VAT rule found).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official law text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tracker, [K] memory. uwi.edu blocked automated reading (403); read via search snippets only.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Data Protection Act No. 5 of 2018 (official Act text, copy hosted by Morrison Foerster). s.1(2): it comes into force only on a day the Minister fixes by Order in the Gazette.
- [V-sec] Not in force: no commencement order published as of Feb 2025 (UWI data protection resource) and none found in 2026 searches (law.ungovr.org tracker). So **no data protection law is in force today.**
- [V-reg] If it starts: it covers private bodies processing data "in respect of commercial transactions" (s.4(1)). A controller not established in SKN is covered only if it uses equipment in SKN for processing (s.4(2)(b)); then it must name a local representative (s.4(3)). An app hosted abroad, with no servers in SKN, would mostly be outside it.
- [V-reg] Consent-based principles for processing and disclosure (ss.7-9), plus a security principle (s.10); regulator would be an Information Commissioner (Part on Information Commissioner). No children's age, no breach-notice deadline, no transfer-abroad rule found in the text.

## Kids and games
- [K] No children's data rule, loot-box or game-time law found. Age of majority is 18. Online gambling needs a local licence; avoid cash-out prizes.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Act No. 21 of 2023, passed 30 Oct 2023: Consumer Affairs Department enforces; labelling, price display and durable receipts (SKNIS government news, InforMEA). Whether it has an online/distance cooling-off right: not confirmed.
- [V-sec] VAT 17% standard rate; imported services are under a reverse charge (paid by the local recipient), no non-resident digital-services registration rule found (expanship, taxratesbycountry). Domestic service threshold reportedly EC$96,000 (lookuptax).
- [K] Stripe does not support SKN-based businesses; selling to SKN customers from abroad via Stripe/Paddle/Lemon Squeezy works. Regional trend: Grenada passed a digital-services VAT bill in 2026, so SKN may follow.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI-specific law; relies on existing IP, cyber and privacy law (Lex Mundi AI guide, Sep 2025). CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap endorsed 18 Jun 2026 (policy, not law) (CPA UK/UNESCO workshop statement).
- [K] Electronic Crimes Act 2009 covers harassment and illegal content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No data law in force, but still write a clear privacy policy and only collect what you need; the 2018 Act could be switched on by a single Gazette order.
- Do not host servers in SKN unless you are ready to name a local representative.
- Normal fair-selling basics: clear price, receipt, refund terms.
- Watch for a digital-services VAT (regional trend).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] DPA 2018 commencement order not yet issued; tracker says it is on the legislative agenda for revision. No date.

## Unverified
- Whether a commencement order was issued after early 2026; contents and online-sale rules of the Consumer Protection Act 2023; VAT treatment of B2C digital imports; current threshold.
