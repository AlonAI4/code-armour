# Iraq (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low legal risk from privacy/tax; High practical risk for payments (sanctions, no Stripe/PayPal) and sudden platform blocks.
Note: no regulator site fetched (CMC not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data-protection law. A draft was approved by cabinet in 2019, re-sent to parliament in 2021, still not enacted. (law.ungovr.org)
- [K] Scattered duties in the Penal Code and the 2012 e-signature/e-transactions law. No breach deadline or children's age.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game or social age law found; no loot-box rule found. [K] Authorities have banned some games/apps before (PUBG-style bans, 2019), not verified here.

## Selling
- [K] Iraq has no general VAT; no digital-services VAT for foreign sellers found. Not confirmed.
- [V-sec] Stripe, PayPal and Shopify Payments do not support Iraq; local gateways (e.g. PayTabs/Amwal) are workarounds. (Shopify community, WMtips)
- [V-sec] Google Play allowed Iraqi users to buy paid apps and in-app items since 2019, but developers based in Iraq still cannot sell. (Android Police)
- [V-sec] US Treasury sanctions notice to Iraqi banks and a reported plan to ban foreign card use for electronic payments. Expect failed or blocked payments. (Shafaq)

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Draft Cybercrime Law has been debated since 2011; no version enacted as of early 2026; criticised as vague on speech. (New Arab, Jummar)
- [V-sec] The Communications and Media Commission blocked Telegram in Apr-May 2026 and lifted it on 9 May 2026 after the platform agreed to comply. Shows blocks can come fast. (Rudaw, TechRadar)
- [K] No AI law.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Treat Iraq like a low-regulation market: basic privacy policy and consent; parent consent for kids as a default.
- Take payments via app stores or a Merchant of Record; do not rely on card payments from Iraqi banks.
- Moderate chat for religion, sectarian and political content.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Cybercrime bill and data-protection bill: pending, no dates.
