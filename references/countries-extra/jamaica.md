# Jamaica (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (post-GDPR law in force, rep requirement; digital GCT coming 2027).
Note: only search snippets read; oic.gov.jm and jamaicatax.gov.jm not opened. [V-sec] = law-firm/news text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2020, fully in force 1 Dec 2023, regulator Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC). Applies to public and private entities including international businesses operating in Jamaica (Irie FM, Akin Gump, Carlton Fields).
- [V-sec] Breach: tell the Commissioner within 72 hours (and affected people). Children: no processing without parent/guardian consent. Data Controller Registration Regulations 2024 govern registration. A controller outside Jamaica must name a "data controller representative" in Jamaica (a Jamaican resident or entity) (Chambers 2026, theartofservice).
- [K] Whether the Act reaches a foreign-only web app with no Jamaican presence is unclear. Penalties up to 4% of turnover or criminal fines [K].

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Parental-consent rule above is the main child rule. [K] No loot-box, game age or social-media age law found. Cybercrimes Act 2015 covers online abuse [K].

## Selling
- [V-sec] 2026-27 Budget (Feb 2026): GCT of 15% on digital services and intangibles supplied from abroad; non-resident providers must register with TAJ and collect on B2C sales; target early 2027, needs enabling legislation, categories (streaming, e-books, software, app-store purchases) not yet defined (EY, KPMG, PayPro).
- [K] Domestic GCT registration threshold is JMD 15M (one source cites it for non-residents; unconfirmed whether it applies). Standard GCT rate 15%.
- [K] Stripe does not generally support Jamaican businesses; PayPal is limited. Consumer Protection Act 2005 applies.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. 

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy, parent consent for children, 72h breach plan, deletion path; if you have real Jamaican user numbers, check OIC registration and the local-representative rule.
- Sell through app stores or a Merchant of Record before GCT begins (expected 2027).
- Watch the GCT legislation text for a threshold.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] GCT on foreign digital services: expected early 2027 after legislation; revenue target JMD 300M in FY2026/27.
