# Senegal (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: foreign digital VAT with no threshold since Jul 2024.
Note: no regulator site fetched (cdp.sn, dgid.sn not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 2008-12 of 25 Jan 2008; regulator CDP. Applies to processing in Senegal and wherever Senegalese law applies. (DLA Piper, Osiris)
- [K] Declaration/authorisation to CDP before processing; consent; transfers abroad to adequate countries; children's age not confirmed; breach deadline not confirmed.
- [V-sec] Jan 2026: government unveiled a draft to modernise the law; still under development. (DLA Piper Africa)

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found. [K] No loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 18%. Art. 355 bis, enforced from 1 Jul 2024: non-resident digital suppliers register online and charge VAT; no threshold, one sale triggers it; B2B reverse charge. (vatcalc, Anrok, Kintsugi)
- [K] Consumer Protection Law 2010-03. Stripe not available for Senegalese sellers; mobile money (Wave, Orange Money) dominates local payments.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Feb 2025 "Technological New Deal" digital strategy plans an AI legal framework and reform of data and cyber law; AI strategy and roadmap exist. No AI law yet. (DLA Piper Africa, OECD.AI)
- [V-sec] The National Assembly adopted a cybersecurity bill no. 25/2026 (title seen only). (NDAR Info)
- [K] 2008 cybercrime law; content offences incl. insulting the head of state; internet shutdowns have happened.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy (French is smart), consent, deletion route; parent consent for kids.
- Selling to Senegalese buyers directly: register for 18% VAT, or use a Merchant of Record or app stores.
- Moderate chat; report button.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data-protection reform draft (Jan 2026) and AI legal framework: no dates.
