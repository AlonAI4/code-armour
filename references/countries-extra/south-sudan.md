# South Sudan (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low (no data protection law; sanctions are targeted; payments mostly don't work).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law in force. The ICT minister said a Data Protection Bill would come in 2026 (to create a Data Protection Commission); civil society (Paradigm Initiative, Koneta Hub) worked on a draft in Oct 2025. No report of it being tabled (Radio Tamazuj, Paradigm Initiative).
- [V-sec] Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2025: passed Nov 2025, signed by President Kiir 18 Feb 2026. Covers hacking, impersonation, online harassment, "economic sabotage"; 2-20 years' prison, fines up to about USD 7,700. Rights groups call it a threat to free speech (Radio Tamazuj, Eye Radio, ITWeb).
- [K] No cookie, breach or data-transfer rules.

## Kids and games
- [K] Child Act 2008: child = under 18. No online-age or loot-box rules.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT reported at 18%; National Revenue Authority e-tax portal since Jan 2024; cross-border/digital rules "not yet published" (e-invoice.app; taxratesbycountry is generic). No digital-VAT regime for foreign sellers found.
- [V-sec] PayPal does NOT list South Sudan as a PayPal country (paypal.com). Not on Stripe's prohibited-jurisdiction list (stripe.com).
- [K] Few international cards; most payments are cash or mobile money. Expect paid sales to fail.

## Sanctions (as of 2026-10-07)
- [V-sec] UN: arms embargo plus targeted travel bans and asset freezes, renewed by Res. 2821 (late May 2026, 9 votes, 6 abstentions) to 31 May 2027; Panel of Experts to 1 Jul 2027 (Anadolu, Radio Tamazuj, Amnesty).
- [V-reg] US: OFAC South Sudan program (EO 13664, 31 CFR 558) blocks **listed** persons only; NGO/agriculture/medicine general licences (OFAC page).
- [V-sec] EU: list-based regime (Reg. 2015/735), amended Jun 2026 (Malta sanctions board / ONPCSB summary).
- Effect for a small app: targeted only, no broad embargo. Free sign-ups are fine; paid sales aren't banned but usually can't be processed. Don't deal with listed people (many are senior officials/military). See `sanctioned-places.md`.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. The new cybercrime act is broad: moderate user content and have a takedown contact if you have users there.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No local privacy law yet: GDPR-style basics are enough.
- Payments won't work for most users (no PayPal).
- Don't knowingly deal with sanctioned officials.
- Watch for the 2026 Data Protection Bill.

## Upcoming
