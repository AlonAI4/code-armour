# North Macedonia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. VAT is the real cost if you sell direct.
Note: no regulator site (dzlp.mk, ujp.gov.mk) was read; all lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law on Personal Data Protection published 16 Feb 2020, in force 24 Feb 2020, fully applicable 24 Aug 2021; substantially GDPR-aligned. (Schoenherr, CMS)
- [V-sec] Reaches foreign controllers that offer goods/services to people in North Macedonia (free or paid) or monitor them there. (CMS / Global Legal Post)
- [V-sec] Children under 14 need a parent's consent. (CMS / Art of Service)
- [K] Breach notice within 72h to the Personal Data Protection Agency; foreign controllers need a local representative. Not confirmed today.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time, loot-box or social media age law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Since 1 Jan 2024, non-resident B2C digital services: 18% VAT from the first sale, no threshold; register with the Public Revenue Office, a local tax representative may be needed; filing monthly or quarterly. (Pay Pro Global; Stripe lists North Macedonia in its tax docs)
- [K] Consumer rules follow the Consumer Protection Law; 14-day withdrawal for distance sales, digital exceptions unverified.
- [K] Stripe does not onboard North Macedonian businesses; merchant of record can sell to buyers there. Not verified.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law and no AI oversight body today. (CMS AI scanner)

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- EU-style privacy; parent consent for under-14s; 72h breach plan; delete/export on request.
- Selling directly triggers 18% VAT from sale one; use a merchant of record or app stores.
- Free app: only the privacy basics.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] AI policy alignment with the EU is discussed (EPI paper) but no dated law found.

## Unverified
- Representative duty, breach deadline, consumer cooling-off, payment providers, regulator text.
