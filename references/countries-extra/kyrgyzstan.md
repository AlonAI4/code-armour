# Kyrgyzstan (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med on paper (new Digital Code + 12% VAT from the first sale), Low in practice for a tiny foreign app.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/NGO/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: cbd.minjust.gov.kg, salyk.kg and dpa.gov.kg were not read directly; lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] The Digital Code (No. 178 of 31 Jul 2025) is now the main privacy law. It came into force on 5 Feb 2026 and replaced the 2008 Law on Personal Information (No. 58), which was repealed by Law No. 179. (ICNL, ICNL guidance note Apr 2026)
- [V-sec] Regulator: State Agency for Personal Data Protection (DPA, dpa.gov.kg). It keeps a public register of incidents, inspections and fines. (ICNL guidance note)
- [V-sec] Registration in the old "Register of Personal Data Holders" is no longer required under the Digital Code. (ICNL guidance note)
- [V-sec] Consent must be voluntary, specific (a separate consent for each purpose), informed and in clear language. Requests for consent from a child must be written so a child can understand them. Minors can consent only within the legal capacity the Civil Code gives them. (ICNL guidance note)
- [K] Civil Code: full capacity at 18; from 14 a minor has partial capacity. Treat under-18s as needing a parent's consent for anything beyond simple use.
- [V-sec] Cross-border transfer (Art. 89): allowed to countries on the DPA's published "adequate" list; otherwise only with explicit consent, for a contract, for vital interests or similar. Illegal transfer is fined under Code on Offenses Art. 413-3. (ICNL guidance note)
- [V-sec] Fines (Code on Offenses Art. 413-2, since 23 Nov 2025): 65,000 KGS for a company, 7,500 KGS for an individual (higher for repeats). Ignoring access/deletion requests: 2,000-5,000 KGS. Small amounts. (ICNL guidance note)
- [V-sec] Foreign reach: no clear GDPR-style extraterritorial clause found. DLA Piper notes the old registration system only fitted local entities. (DLA Piper, OfflistMe)
- [K] No breach-notification deadline found in the sources read.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] 2023 law "On measures to prevent harm to the health of children" (harmful information). It was used to block TikTok from Apr 2024; the block was still reported in Jun 2026. (Eurasianet, RFE/RL, CADE)
- [K] No social media age limit or loot-box law found. Gambling is restricted; keep cash-out items out.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Since 1 Jan 2022: foreign sellers of electronic services to Kyrgyz individuals must register for VAT at 12% through the State Tax Service online portal. No threshold for non-residents. No local input-VAT reclaim. (VATCalc, Gratanet/Mondaq, Orbitax)
- [V-sec] Customer location evidence: billing address, bank/card country, IP address, phone country code. Returns quarterly; sources disagree on the due day (20th vs 25th). (VATCalc, Gratanet)
- [K] Consumer law (Law "On protection of consumer rights") gives a 14-day exchange/return for non-food goods of proper quality, with a Cabinet list of exceptions (Resolution No. 313 of 13 Jun 2024 per Economist.kg). Digital content is not clearly covered.
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] The Digital Code has a section on AI systems but takes a light-touch, self-regulation approach (industry self-regulatory organisations set standards). No EU-style AI rules. (Eurasianet, Babl.ai)
- [K] Authorities can block sites (TikTok example); moderate user content for "harmful to children" material.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Get specific, purpose-by-purpose consent; write it in plain language; ask a parent for under-18s.
- If you sell to Kyrgyz consumers: 12% VAT from sale 1, or use a Merchant of Record.
- Say in your privacy notice which countries data goes to (hosting abroad is a cross-border transfer).
- Fines are small, but the DPA publishes cases.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] The DPA is still issuing implementing rules and guidance for the Digital Code (2026). (ICNL)
