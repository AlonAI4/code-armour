# São Tomé and Príncipe (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app: a GDPR-era-style law (2016) with a regulator, but it only reaches foreign apps that use servers or equipment in the country.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory. Law read in the French translation hosted by AFAPDP (association of francophone data authorities), not the Portuguese gazette.

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Law No. 03/2016 on personal data protection (dated 10 May 2016 per parliament records [V-sec]). Regulator: ANPDP (Agência Nacional de Protecção de Dados Pessoais); organised by Law 7/2017 [V-sec].
- [V-reg] Applies to controllers established in STP, and to foreign controllers that use means in STP (incl. hosting data on media in the country), excluding mere transit; those must name a representative in STP via the ANPDP (Art. 3).
- [V-reg] Controllers must notify the ANPDP in writing at least 8 days before starting processing (Art. 21); ANPDP can simplify or exempt some categories. [V-sec] 2018 ANPDP resolutions exempted some notifications, mainly employee data (DataGuidance).
- [V-reg] Transfers abroad only to places with adequate protection; otherwise with unambiguous consent or other exceptions, by notification or ANPDP authorisation.
- [V-reg] Fines are set in dobras: e.g. 50-120 million for individuals and up to 500 million for companies. [K] These look like pre-2018 dobras (STD); after the 2018 redenomination (1,000 old = 1 new) that is about STN 50,000-500,000.
- [V-sec] No breach notification, DPO or DPIA duty (DataGuidance).

## Kids and games
- [V-reg] No age of digital consent found in the law. [K] Civil majority is 18; parent consent under 18 to be safe.
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT (IVA) replaced the old consumption tax on 1 Jun 2023; standard rate 15% (VATCalc, Orbitax).
- [V-sec] Non-residents without a permanent establishment must appoint a local representative unless they use a simplified registration regime (VdA 2019 briefing on the draft).
- [K] No specific rule for foreign digital services to consumers confirmed. Stripe doesn't support sellers in STP.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Portuguese or French privacy notice, consent, deletion route; parent consent under 18.
- If you host nothing in STP, the data law likely doesn't apply to you directly.
- Paid features: a Merchant of Record handles any VAT.
