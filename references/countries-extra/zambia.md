# Zambia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: controller registration is mandatory and fines are real; foreign VAT above a threshold.
Note: no regulator site fetched (zra.org.zm failed with a certificate error; ODPC not read). [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2021 (passed 23 Mar 2021); Office of the Data Protection Commissioner now operating. Every controller/processor must register; deadline 30 Apr 2025; failing is an offence (fine ZMW 200,000 and/or up to 5 years). Companies can also face up to 2% of turnover. (Bowmans, itedgenews, Securiti)
- [V-sec] Breach: tell the Commissioner within 24 hours. (Securiti)
- [V-sec] Children: processing needs parent/guardian consent; controller must use age verification and parental-consent mechanisms. (Securiti, Act text)
- [K] Foreign reach and exact small-app registration duty not confirmed; cross-border transfers need adequacy or safeguards.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 16%. VAT (Cross Border Electronic Services) Regulations since April 2024: non-resident e-service suppliers register with ZRA, charge 16%, file monthly via a simplified portal or local agent. Threshold ZMW 800,000/yr or ZMW 200,000/quarter. (vatcalc, Kintsugi, Payproglobal)
- [K] Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2010. Stripe not available for Zambian sellers; mobile money common.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Cyber Security Act and Cyber Crimes Act signed 8 Apr 2025, replacing the 2021 laws; broad extraterritorial reach where conduct has effects in Zambia; rights groups raised speech concerns. (Afriwise, GNI, TechPolicy.Press)
- [K] No AI law.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy, consent, parent consent for kids with some age check, deletion route, 24h breach plan.
- Consider registering with the ODPC if you hold Zambian users' data at scale; risk is low for tiny apps but the penalty is real.
- Stay under ZMW 800,000/yr Zambian sales or use a Merchant of Record.
- Moderate chat; report button.
