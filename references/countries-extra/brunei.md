# Brunei (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med on paper (new PDPO in force 2026: under-18 parental consent, 3-day breach notice, high fines), Low in practice for a tiny foreign app. No sales tax.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: the PDPO gazette text (AGC) was not read; the aiti.gov.bn data-protection page returned 404. AITI's AI guide page was read.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Order 2025 (PDPO), made 8 Jan 2025; main parts in force 1 Jan 2026 after a one-year sunrise. Covers private-sector organisations and NGOs, not government. Regulator: AITI. (DLA Piper, updated 18 Mar 2026; The Scoop; AITI event page)
- [V-sec] Applies to organisations whether or not they have a physical presence or registration in Brunei. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Consent first (express); deemed consent or exceptions only in limited cases. Consent is invalid if forced as a condition beyond what the service needs. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Under 18: a parent or legal guardian must consent. AITI plans guidance on minors. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Breach: notify the regulator as soon as practicable and within 3 calendar days of assessing a breach that causes significant harm or is of significant scale; tell affected people if significant harm. Industry asked for working days and a 500-person threshold; final outcome not confirmed. (DLA Piper; AITI consultation submissions)
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad only with comparable protection; AITI recommends the ASEAN Model Contractual Clauses. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Fines up to BND 1 million, or 10% of Brunei turnover for larger firms. (DLA Piper, ConsentStack)

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] No social media age law found (unlike Malaysia's under-16 rule from 2026). (search, Sep 2026)
- [K] Gambling is banned (Syariah Penal Code / Common Gaming Houses Act). Treat paid loot boxes with cash-out value as gambling; avoid.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No GST, VAT or sales tax; no tax on digital services from abroad. (TaxAtlas 2026, The Scoop Mar 2024)
- [K] Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Order 2011: no misleading claims; no online cooling-off period known.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-reg] AITI "Guide on AI Governance and Ethics for Brunei Darussalam", 11 Apr 2025: seven principles (transparency, data protection, safety, robustness, fairness, human centricity, accountability). A baseline guide, not a law. (aiti.gov.bn)
- [K] Strict content rules (Syariah Penal Code, sedition). Moderate user content on religion and the monarchy.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Ask for a parent's consent for anyone under 18 (or block under-18s).
- Have a breach plan that can notify within 3 days.
- Use a standard contract (e.g. ASEAN clauses) with your hosting provider; say where data is stored.
- No tax registration needed for selling.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] AITI guidance on minors' data and breach thresholds expected. DPO requirement expected. (DLA Piper)
