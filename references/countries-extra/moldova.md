# Moldova (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low-Med for a small foreign-run app. New privacy law only started applying on 23 Aug 2026, so enforcement practice is untested.
Note: no regulator site (datepersonale.md, fisc.md) was read; lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 195/2024 on personal data protection: adopted 25 Jul 2024, published 23 Aug 2024, applies from 23 Aug 2026 (now in force). Partly GDPR-based but with differences. (Clym, Logos Press, Consentmo)
- [V-sec] Explicit consent required, including for cookies. Fines capped at 1% of turnover (not GDPR's 4%). (Consentmo, Clym)
- [V-sec] Breach: notify the National Centre for Personal Data Protection within 72h, unless risk is very low. (Logos Press)
- [K] Reaches foreign controllers targeting Moldovan residents (GDPR-style); local representative duty likely. Children's consent age not confirmed (16 is my guess).

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Since 1 Apr 2020, non-resident B2C digital services: 20% VAT from the first sale, no threshold, via the simplified "e-Commerce-VAT" portal. B2B uses reverse charge. (Pay Pro Global, Anrok, Stripe docs list Moldova)
- [K] Stripe does not onboard Moldovan businesses; merchant of record can sell to buyers there.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law. National AI Programme 2026-2030 concept being drafted to align with the EU AI Act; an inter-ministerial AI sub-council was set up Apr 2026. (Logos Press)

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- EU-style privacy: explicit consent (also for cookies), deletion, 72h breach plan, parent consent for minors.
- Selling direct means 20% VAT from sale one via the e-portal; or use a merchant of record.
- Free app: privacy basics only.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] EU-AI-Act-aligned AI rules planned (no date). Details of Law 195/2024 secondary acts may still be issued.

## Unverified
