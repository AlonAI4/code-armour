## Privacy
- [V-sec] No national data-protection law in force and no data-protection authority found. Sources mention a draft bill only; none shows it passed (law.ungovr.org, generisonline). Not confirmed on any Guinea-Bissau government site.
- [V-sec] Regional rule: as an ECOWAS member, Guinea-Bissau is covered by the ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on Personal Data Protection (2010), which tells member states to pass a national law and set up an authority. It has no direct enforcer here. ECOWAS consulted on revising the Act (15 Oct to 30 Dec 2024); no revised Act found (NEPAD consultation page).
- [K] Constitution protects private life. Without a law, there is no consent age, breach deadline or transfer rule to follow.
- Practical: follow your GDPR-style baseline (privacy policy, minimal data, delete on request). That covers you if a law arrives.

## Kids and games
- [K] Age of majority 18. No children's data, loot-box or game-time rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT (IVA) law published in the Boletim Oficial 25 Feb 2022; the tax office said VAT starts 1 Jan 2025, replacing the old IGV sales tax (Forbes Africa Lusófona).
- [V-sec] No rule found on VAT for foreign sellers of digital services to consumers here. Treat as "not confirmed", not "no tax".
- [K] UEMOA member; UEMOA rules set national VAT rates in a 15-20% band. Rate here not confirmed.
- [K] No consumer-protection law with an online refund window found.
- Practical: a Merchant of Record covers any VAT change.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Thin digital law framework; no dedicated cybercrime law reported (law.ungovr.org). No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No local privacy law to comply with today; keep your normal privacy baseline.
- Use a Merchant of Record if you sell; the VAT system is new (2025).
- Recheck once a year: a bill exists and ECOWAS is revising its data-protection Act.
