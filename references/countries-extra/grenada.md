General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2023 (Act No. 1 of 2023), assented 9 May 2023, gazetted 10 May 2023 with no date of effect (NOW Grenada, DataGuidance).
- [V-sec] Needs a Ministerial commencement order; none found, and a 2026 Government of Grenada document calls the framework "not in force" (Offlist, Sep 2026; one source). Treat as no data protection law in force.
- [V-sec] Regulator would be an Information/Data Protection Commission; none appointed found (DataGuidance, Offlist).
- [K] Child age, breach deadline and foreign reach of the 2023 Act not confirmed. No cookie law.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box, game-time or kids' online law.

## Selling
- [K] VAT standard rate 15%.
- [V-reg] Value Added Tax (Amendment) Bill 2026: non-resident suppliers of digital services to consumers in Grenada must register, via a simplified regime set by Regulations (no input tax credit) or normal registration (new s.9A). Subscriptions: each payment is a separate supply. Starts on a date set by notice; 6-month window after start to register (s.11).
- [V-sec] Passed Parliament 7 May 2026, awaiting Governor-General assent. Scope: streaming, online ads, cloud/SaaS, software, e-learning. B2B: reverse charge. Consumer is "in Grenada" if 2+ indicators (billing address, IP, bank) say so. Marketplaces liable as supplier. No threshold for non-residents in the bill (KPMG, VATCalc).
- [K] Consumer law: general; no online cooling-off right found.
- Practical: Merchant of Record (Paddle / Lemon Squeezy) will handle it once it starts.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law found; CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap is non-binding (Jun 2026).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No privacy law in force: general good practice (privacy policy, delete on request, parent consent for young kids).
- If you sell digital products, plan for Grenada VAT (likely 2027); a Merchant of Record avoids registering yourself.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Digital-services VAT on non-residents: start date by Gazette notice after assent; 6-month registration window. VATCalc expects 2027.
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2023: commencement order, date unknown.
