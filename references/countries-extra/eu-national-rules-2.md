# EU national rules 2: Ireland, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Belgium, Austria, Portugal, Czechia, Romania, Greece, Hungary (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
What each country ADDS on top of EU-wide law. GDPR, ePrivacy cookie consent, DSA, AI Act, EU VAT/OSS, the 14-day withdrawal right, the EAA, CRA and PLD are in references/legal.md; Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands and Poland are in eu-national-rules.md. Neither is repeated here. VAT: all eleven use EU OSS, so there are no national VAT thresholds for a foreign digital seller to track; a Merchant of Record removes the problem.

Severity for a small foreign-run app with a few users: Belgium Med-High (only if your game has paid loot boxes), Ireland Low-Med (High if you are a video-sharing platform), Austria Med (Impressum even for private sites), Czechia Med (cookie fines), Romania/Hungary/Greece Low-Med, Sweden/Denmark/Finland/Portugal Low.

Digital-consent ages (GDPR Art. 8) [V-sec] https://childreninthedigitalworld.twobirds.com/home/eu : 13 = BE, DK, FI, PT, SE; 14 = AT; 15 = CZ, GR; 16 = IE, RO, HU.

## Ireland
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 16. [V-reg] The DPC also fines small organisations, not just Big Tech: smallest published fines are EUR 1,500 (an NGO), EUR 5,000 (a credit union), EUR 15,000 (a courier firm). https://dataprotection.ie/en/dpc-guidance/decisions/fines
- [V-sec] DPC 2025: EUR 530.77M in fines, EUR 530M of it TikTok (children's data transfers). https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/data-privacy-and-cyber-risk/dpc-annual-report-2025-key-takeaways
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2018 s.30 (ban on profiling/direct marketing to children) was passed but NEVER commenced, so it does not apply today. https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/data-privacy-and-cyber-risk/gdpr-and-children
- [K] DPC "Fundamentals for a Child-Oriented Approach" (Dec 2021): its guidance expects high privacy by default, no nudging, and no profiling of under-18s for ads. Not binding law, but it is how the DPC judges apps that kids use.
- Platforms: [V-reg] Coimisiún na Meán's Online Safety Code applies only to DESIGNATED video-sharing platforms (services whose main purpose or essential feature is user videos). They must have terms that ban harmful content, age assurance, parental controls and reporting tools. https://www.cnam.ie/online-safety-code/
- [V-sec] Part B (age assurance for porn and gratuitous violence, where self-declaration is NOT enough) binding since 21 Jul 2025; fines up to EUR 20M or 10% of turnover. Designated: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Tumblr, Udemy. A small app will not be designated, but an Irish-based video app could be. https://www.yoti.com/blog/ireland-online-safety-code-how-platforms-can-comply/
- [K] Coimisiún na Meán is also Ireland's DSA coordinator, so it supervises the many platforms headquartered in Ireland.
- Business info: [K] Irish companies must show their registered name, number and office on the website (Companies Act 2014). Consumer Rights Act 2022 bans unfair terms and gives digital-content remedies similar to EU law.

## Sweden
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 13. [K] IMY (DPA) mostly targets larger organisations; cookies are enforced by PTS under the Electronic Communications Act (LEK), and enforcement is rare.
- Kids and games: [V-sec] On 3 Sep 2026 the government ordered an inquiry into gambling harm among young people. It also covers lottery/casino-style features in video games (loot boxes) and whether children need more protection. No rules yet. https://www.casinos.com/news/sweden-launches-inquiry-into-gambling-harm-among-young-men
- [V-sec] Earlier Konsumentverket report on loot boxes; very few public complaints. https://www.pocketgamer.biz/sweden-to-review-consumer-protection-measures-against-loot-boxes
- Selling: [K] The Consumer Ombudsman (KO) enforces marketing rules strictly (hidden ads, influencer labelling, "free" claims). No rule that terms must be in Swedish, though it is expected for consumers.
- Accessibility: [K] EAA implemented by the Act on certain products and services' accessibility (2023:254), in force 28 Jun 2025; PTS supervises e-commerce.

## Denmark
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 13. [K] Datatilsynet cannot fine directly: it reports cases to the police and courts set the fine, so fines are slower and rarer. The Danish Business Authority enforces the cookie order.
- Kids: [V-sec] Political agreement (7 Nov 2025) to ban social media for under-15s, with parents able to allow access from 13; could become law "as soon as mid-2026". In-force date not confirmed. https://abc7news.com/post/denmarks-government-aims-ban-access-social-media-children-15/18127417/
- AI/deepfakes: [V-reg] Copyright Act amendment giving every person a right over their own face, body and voice: sharing a realistic AI imitation without consent becomes unlawful (parody/satire excepted), protected until 50 years after death. Notified to the EU (TRIS 2025/0654/DK), expected in force 1 Jul 2026; final adoption not confirmed. https://intellectual-property-helpdesk.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/denmarks-proposed-copyright-rules-deepfakes-2026-06-01_en ; https://eprs.europarl.europa.eu/contents/publications/EPRS/2026/01/EPRS_ATA(2026)782611.html
- What it means: an AI app that lets users make face-swaps or voice clones of real people needs a consent check and fast takedown for Danish users.
- Selling: [K] The Consumer Ombudsman (Forbrugerombudsmanden) is active on in-game purchases and published guidance on games aimed at children (no direct "buy now" pressure on kids).

## Finland
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 13. [K] Sanctions board of the Data Protection Ombudsman issues fines; mostly larger firms.
- Cookies: [V-sec] Traficom (not the DPA) enforces cookies under s.205 of the Act on Electronic Communications Services. Its 2021 guidance requires active opt-in before any non-essential cookie, and it has issued decisions against companies; Finnish courts have backed it. https://traficom.fi/en/regulations/guidance-use-web-cookies-service-providers ; https://finland.dlapiper.com/en/news/recent-court-rulings-emphasise-cookie-legislation-enforcement-finland
- Games: [K] Finland's lotteries act is being reformed (licensing opens 2027); loot boxes are not classed as gambling today.
- Selling/language: [K] No strict language-of-terms law for foreign sellers; the Consumer Ombudsman (KKV) acts on marketing to children and in-game purchases.

## Belgium
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 13. [K] The Belgian DPA (APD/GBA) is strict on cookies: it ruled against IAB Europe's consent framework (2022) and fines small firms too, often a few thousand euros.
- Loot boxes: [V-sec] Paid loot boxes are ILLEGAL GAMBLING in Belgium since the Gaming Commission's April 2018 report. Penalties up to EUR 800,000 and 5 years in prison, doubled if minors are involved. EA stopped selling FIFA Points there in 2019. https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2025/03/an-iphone-a-gambling-problem-and-the-loot-box-debate
- [V-sec] Antwerp Enterprise Court, 16 Jan 2025 (LS v Apple): a player spent EUR 67,813 on loot boxes in Top War. The court held loot boxes are illegal gambling and that Apple broke gambling law by offering the game in its Belgian App Store; it referred questions on damages to the CJEU. Platforms may now block such games. https://www.vixio.com/insights/gc-belgium-loot-box-ruling-could-be-bad-news-digital-platforms
- [V-sec] In practice enforcement is weak: a study found 82% of the top-100 grossing iPhone games in Belgium still had paid random rewards. Don't rely on that. https://pure.itu.dk/en/publications/breaking-ban-belgiums-ineffective-gambling-law-regulation-of-vide/
- What to do: no paid random rewards for Belgian users. Geo-block the feature, sell items directly, or make rewards earnable only through play.
- Language: [K] Belgian language laws apply mainly to employment and public bodies; consumer info should be in the language of the user's region (Dutch/French/German). Clear terms in the user's language are the safe choice.
- Business info: [K] Belgian businesses must show their enterprise number (KBO/BCE) on the website.

## Austria
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 14. [K] The DSB (DPA) can fine; noyb (Max Schrems's NGO) is based in Vienna and files many complaints, including cookie complaints.
- Imprint: [V-sec] Austria has TWO imprint rules: § 5 E-Commerce Act (for commercial sites) and § 25 Media Act "Offenlegung" (disclosure), which applies to ALL websites, including private ones. Small sites need at least name and place of residence/registered office. https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/oesterreich-impressum.html ; https://prod.wko.at/oe/internetrecht/das-korrekte-website-impressum.pdf
- Loot boxes: [V-sec] Supreme Court (OGH), 18 Dec 2025, 6 Ob 228/24h: FIFA Ultimate Team packs are NOT gambling because the game must be judged as a whole (skill matters). This overturned lower-court orders to refund about EUR 20,000. Pure luck-based loot boxes with cash-out could still be argued differently. https://esportslegal.news/2026/01/28/austrian-supreme-court-loot-boxes/
- Kids: [V-sec] Coalition announced (27 Mar 2026) a social media ban for under-14s on platforms with addictive algorithms; draft law was due by end of June 2026; needs parliament; no in-force date. https://wtop.com/lifestyle/2026/03/austria-plans-to-ban-social-media-use-for-under-14s-joining-a-string-of-other-countries/
- [K] KommAustria (RTR) is the DSA coordinator.

## Portugal
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 13. [K] CNPD rarely fines small foreign apps.
- Kids: [V-sec] Bill passed first reading 12 Feb 2026: under-13s banned from social networks, video/image-sharing platforms and betting sites; 13-15 need parental consent via the state Digital Mobile Key (Chave Móvel Digital); platforms must use age verification compatible with it. Final vote / in-force date not confirmed. https://euronews.com/2026/02/13/portugal-green-lights-restrictions-for-social-media-access-for-children-under-16 ; https://merlin.obs.coe.int/article/10492
- Selling: [K] Online sellers to Portuguese consumers must link to the electronic Complaints Book (Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico) on their site; contracts and consumer info must be in Portuguese (Decree-Law 238/86).

## Czechia
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 15.
- Cookies: [V-sec] Since 1 Jan 2022 the Electronic Communications Act requires opt-in consent (previously opt-out). The DPA (ÚOOÚ) made cookies its 2022 focus and began fining in 2023; it collected about CZK 4.5M in cookie fines in one year. https://complianz.io/czech-republic-now-requires-opt-in-consent-for-cookies/ ; https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/za-cookies-bez-dostatecneho-souhlasu-urad-na-pokutach-letos-vybral-uz-ctyri-a-pul-milionu-korun/
- Selling: [K] The Czech Trade Inspection Authority (ČOI) checks e-shops for fake discounts and missing withdrawal info and fines small shops; consumer info should be in Czech.
- Games: [K] No loot-box rule; the gambling act does not cover in-game items without cash-out.

## Romania
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 16 (Law 190/2018 implements GDPR). [K] ANSPDCP (DPA) issues many small fines (often a few thousand euros) against local businesses, frequently over security breaches and CCTV.
- AI/deepfakes: [V-sec] Draft law on responsible AI registered in the Senate 31 Mar 2025: platforms must label AI-generated content; deepfakes meant to manipulate public opinion banned; 2-5 years' prison for AI used to violate fundamental rights. Not confirmed as passed. https://cms.law/en/rou/legal-updates/romanian-senate-reviews-draft-law-regulating-ai ; https://rri.ro/actualitate/in-actualitate/anti-deepfake-regulations-id617703.html
- Selling: [K] ANPC (consumer authority) is aggressive with fines on e-shops; consumer information must be in Romanian (Law 500/2004 on use of the Romanian language).

## Greece
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 15. [K] HDPA (DPA) fines mostly local firms and the public sector.
- Kids: [V-sec] Under-15 social media ban announced 8 Apr 2026, to be legislated in summer 2026, platforms must comply from 1 Jan 2027. Covers any service where users create profiles, post, comment, like or follow. Platforms must use reliable age verification and re-verify every six months; parents use the state Kids Wallet app to block access at device level. Fines via the DSA. https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/greece-to-impose-social-media-ban-for-children-under-15 ; https://www.tovima.com/society/kidswallet-to-enforce-social-media-limits-for-children/amp/
- What it means: a game or app with profiles and a feed/comments could count as "social media" under that wide definition.
- Selling: [K] Consumer info in Greek is expected; no extra national quirk found.

## Hungary
- Privacy: [V-sec] Consent age 16. [K] NAIH (DPA) is active and fines small domestic businesses, often for ignored access requests and CCTV.
- Games: [V-sec] No specific loot-box law; loot boxes have only been discussed under national gambling rules. Online casino-style games are tightly restricted (concession only). https://mondaq.com/gaming/1311718/loot-boxes-still-a-mystery-in-cee ; https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-online-gambling-regulation-in-europe/hungary
- Content: [K] The 2021 child protection law restricts showing content that "promotes" LGBTQ topics to under-18s; the EU Commission took Hungary to the CJEU over it. Relevant to content/ad targeting, not most small apps.
- Selling: [K] Consumer contract info must be in Hungarian; NMHH is the DSA coordinator.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Ask for birth year and apply the right consent age: 13 BE/DK/FI/PT/SE, 14 AT, 15 CZ/GR, 16 IE/RO/HU. Simplest: treat under-16s as needing a parent EU-wide.
- Belgium: switch off paid loot boxes for Belgian users (criminal law, and Apple can be liable).
- Austria: add a short imprint (name + city) even on a free, private site.
- Czechia/Finland/Belgium: real opt-in cookie banner, no trackers before consent.
- AI face/voice features: consent of the person shown, fast takedown (Denmark likeness right, Romania draft, Italy's crime in the first file).

- Mid-2026 onward (unconfirmed): Denmark under-15 social media ban law.
- 1 Jan 2027: Greece under-15 social media ban (platforms must age-verify).
- Undated: Austria under-14 ban; Portugal 13/16 social media law with Digital Mobile Key; Romania AI/deepfake law; Sweden loot-box/gambling inquiry (started 3 Sep 2026); CJEU answer in Belgium's LS v Apple loot-box case.
