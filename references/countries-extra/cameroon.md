# Cameroon (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: new privacy law just took effect, authority not yet running; foreign VAT plus a new 2026 income tax rule.
Note: no regulator site fetched. [V-sec] = secondary page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law No. 2024/017 of 23 Dec 2024 on personal data protection, first comprehensive law; 18-month compliance period ended 23 Jun 2026 so it now applies. (LexAfrica, Droit Media Finance)
- [V-sec] Covers Cameroon-based controllers and processing where Cameroonian law applies. Needs consent, transparency, security; cross-border transfer only to adequate countries or with approval (Art. 32). (Afriwise, TechHive)
- [V-sec] Minors (under 18): parent or guardian consent where consent is the legal basis. (global center AI)
- [V-sec] Art. 53 creates an independent authority by presidential decree; no decree or complaint portal found as of Aug 2026. Fines up to XAF 1 billion and jail for executives are reported. (itweb, DLA Piper)
- [K] Breach deadline, DPO duty, small-business exemption: not confirmed.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or social age law found; no loot-box rule found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 19.25%. Since Jan 2020 non-resident digital suppliers and platforms must register and charge VAT; no threshold. (Anrok, vatcalc)
- [V-sec] From 1 Jan 2026 (Finance Law 2026): non-resident digital providers of sufficient scale face a minimum 3% tax on Cameroon revenue, or may choose corporate tax on actual profit (~30%). "Sufficient scale" threshold not found. (Digital Business Africa)
- [K] Stripe not available for Cameroon-based sellers; mobile money (MTN, Orange) common.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] 2010 cybersecurity and cybercrime law covers online content; no AI law.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy policy (French and English), consent, parent consent under 18, deletion route, no transfers without safeguards.
- Selling to Cameroonians: Merchant of Record or app stores; direct selling at scale means VAT plus the 3% rule.
- Moderate chat; report button.
