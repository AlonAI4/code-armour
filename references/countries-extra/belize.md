# Belize (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low. A data protection law was passed in 2021 but is not in force and has no regulator; no digital-services tax rule for foreign sellers found.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Data Protection Act 2021 (Act No. 45 of 2021), assented 29 Nov 2021, gazetted 30 Nov 2021. It starts only on a date the Minister sets by Gazette order (s.97); no commencement order found as of Aug 2026 (DataGuidance, Offlist).
- [V-sec] Regulator (Data Protection Commissioner) not yet established (DataGuidance, MoFo privacy library). So: no data protection law in force today.
- [V-sec] One vendor (Clym) claims it took effect on gazettal; this conflicts with s.97 and the regulator trackers, so ignore it.
- [K] The 2021 Act is GDPR/UK-style (consent, rights, breach notice, transfer limits). Details not confirmed (official PDF unreachable).
- [K] No cookie law.

## Kids and games
- [K] No children's online privacy age, loot-box or game-time law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] General Sales Tax (GST) 12.5%, run by the Belize Tax Service (Expanship, Healy Consultants).
- [V-reg] Services supplied to non-residents outside Belize are zero-rated (BTS Public Ruling 04/2023, from 1 Dec 2023). This is about Belizean sellers; it does not create a tax on foreign sellers.
- [V-sec] GST (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act 34 of 2024, from 1 Jan 2025) prepares e-invoicing. No rule found making foreign digital sellers register for GST.
- [K] Consumer protection is weak/general; no online cooling-off right found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law found; CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap is non-binding (Jun 2026).

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No privacy law to comply with today, but follow good practice (privacy policy, delete on request) so you're ready when the 2021 Act starts.
- No Belize-specific tax step found for foreign digital sellers; a Merchant of Record covers you if that changes.
