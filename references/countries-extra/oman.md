# Oman (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app with Omani users: the privacy law is now fully enforceable and VAT has no threshold for foreigners.
Note: no regulator site fetched (MTCIT, tms.gov.om not read). [V-sec] = law firm / vendor page seen today; [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Law, Royal Decree 6/2022; Executive Regulations by Ministerial Decision 34/2024; fully enforceable from 5 Feb 2026 after the transition. Regulator: Ministry of Transport, Communications and IT (MTCIT). (CMS)
- [V-sec] Duties: explicit consent, privacy notices, rights handling, DPO, cross-border controls, breach notice. (CMS)
- [V-sec] Children: explicit consent of the guardian, minimum data, guardian can access/edit, no third-party sharing without guardian consent. (Addleshaw Goddard, Tamimi)
- [V-sec] Breach posing serious/high risk: notify MTCIT and the person within 72 hours (Reg. Art. 30). (Tamimi)
- [V-sec] Transfers abroad need express consent unless anonymised, and an adequate level of protection (Reg. Art. 37).
- [K] Exact reach over foreign developers not confirmed; no small-business exemption seen.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game age/time law found. Content rules on religion and morals are strict.

## Selling
- [V-sec] VAT 5%. Non-resident sellers of digital services to Omani consumers must register from the first B2C sale (no threshold) and may need a tax representative. (Anrok, Quaderno, Kintsugi)
- [K] Consumer Protection Law RD 66/2014. Stripe not available for Oman-based sellers; use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law; Cyber Crime Law RD 12/2011 covers online content. No takedown deadline found.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Privacy notice, explicit consent, guardian consent for under-18s, 72h breach plan, consent before sending data out of Oman.
- Selling digital goods to Omanis directly = register for 5% VAT; a Merchant of Record handles it.
- Moderate chat; report button.
