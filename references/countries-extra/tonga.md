# Tonga (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low today, Med once the Privacy Act starts: it reaches foreign apps that target Tonga and needs parent consent for under-18s.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: paclii.org blocked (Cloudflare). The Privacy Act and Consumer Protection Act PDFs were read on ago.gov.to (Attorney General's Office).

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Privacy Act 2025 (Act 34 of 2025), assented 16 Dec 2025. NOT in force yet: s.1(2) says it starts "on a date proclaimed by Cabinet". No proclamation found; Privacy Commission "not yet established" (MoFo); September 2026 profile found no Commissioner (OffListMe).
- [V-reg] Reach (s.5): applies to controllers established in Tonga, processing in Tonga, or processing linked to "targeted offering of goods or services" to people in Tonga or monitoring their behaviour there. So it reaches foreign apps that target Tonga.
- [V-reg] Children (s.3, s.29): "child" = under 18. If you rely on consent, you need a parent's or guardian's consent, and must use "appropriate mechanisms" to verify age and consent (the Act mentions government ID as an example).
- [V-reg] Breach (s.37): processor tells controller within 72 hours; controller tells the Commission within 72 hours if there's risk to people, and tells affected people without undue delay.
- [V-reg] Transfers abroad (s.39): only with Commission consent, adequate protection (law, contract clauses, binding rules, certification), or exceptions such as the user's informed consent or performing a contract with the user.
- [V-reg] Penalties (s.54): organisations up to TOP 30,000 first time, TOP 100,000 after; individuals up to TOP 5,000 / 30,000.
- [V-reg] Also passed: Personal Health Information Protection Act 2025 (ago.gov.to list).
- [K] No cookie-specific rule.

## Kids and games
- [K] No social media age law or loot-box law found. Gambling is restricted; keep prizes with no cash value.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Consumption tax 15% (Consumption Tax Act 2003). Section 15 is a "reverse charge on supply of foreign services", so local business buyers account for it. No rule found making foreign digital sellers register for sales to consumers (PacLII heading via search; vendor sites).
- [V-reg] Consumer Protection Act 2025 (Act 39 of 2025), assented 16 Dec 2025, starts on a date proclaimed by Cabinet. Has guarantees on goods and services (acceptable quality, fit for purpose, as described), refunds for rejected goods, unfair contract terms void, bans on misleading conduct. Not on the AG's "Not in Force" list, but no commencement notice found either.
- [K] No cooling-off right for online purchases found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law. Tonga passed cybersecurity legislation in 2024 (nationalityforall.org says so; not checked). No online-safety takedown law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Build to the Privacy Act now: it is passed and could start any time.
- Under-18 users in Tonga: get parent consent (or don't collect their data).
- Have a 72-hour breach plan.
- Using US/EU hosting: rely on user consent or contract necessity, or standard contract clauses with your host.
- Sell through a Merchant of Record.
