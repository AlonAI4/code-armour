# Uruguay (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (EU-adequate privacy regime, active regulator, VAT with no threshold).
Note: only search snippets read; gub.uy/URCDP and DGI pages were not opened. [V-sec] = law-firm/news text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law 18.331 (in force 11 Aug 2008), regulator URCDP. EU adequacy since 2012 (Cookie-Script, EC IP helpdesk).
- [V-sec] Applies when processing uses means located in Uruguay, or a controller established there. A foreign-only web app is a grey area; Law 20.075/20.212 amendments widened reach [K, not confirmed].
- [V-sec] Breach: notify URCDP and affected people within 72 hours of confirming the breach (cookie-script, abogados.com.ar). Fines up to 500,000 UI (about USD 60k); warnings, suspension and database closure possible.
- [K] Consent must be free, prior, express, informed. Children: age not confirmed; use parental consent under 18 for safety.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game age limit or loot-box law found. [V-sec] Bill on AI-generated sexual content (amends Law 19.580 art. 92) passed the Chamber 17 Dec 2025, Senate committee 21 Apr 2026, plenary vote pending.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident providers of audiovisual and intermediation services must register and charge IVA from 1 Jan 2018 with no threshold (PwC Uruguay, PayPro). [V-sec] DGI Consultation 6679 (1 Oct 2025): Swiss subscription with audiovisual content taxed. Quarterly filing. B2B: reverse charge by Uruguayan buyer. [K] Rate 22%. Whether games/apps fall under "audiovisual" is unclear; platforms such as app stores usually handle it.
- [V-sec] Consumer Relations Law 17.250 (2000); Law 20.212 (in force 1 Jan 2024) changed auto-renewal rules: cancel within 60 days after an auto-renewal, provider has 15 days to process (abogados.com.ar). [K] 5-day distance-sale withdrawal right exists; not confirmed for digital goods.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law; a bill is being discussed (Panamericana, Pulitzer Center, 2026). Uruguay's AI governance is by strategy and guidance.
- [K] No notice-and-takedown deadline found.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Plain-language privacy policy (Spanish is better), deletion/export, 72h breach plan.
- Subscriptions: easy cancel, and let users cancel after a renewal.
- Selling directly to Uruguayans: use a Merchant of Record or app stores; direct sales of audiovisual-type content need IVA registration.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Senate vote on AI sexual-content bill; AI regulation bill under discussion.

## Unverified
