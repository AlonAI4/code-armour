# Federated States of Micronesia (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No general data protection law in force. Only telecom rules: 21 FSMC 349 (telecom providers need customer consent to collect, use or disclose customer data, plus security safeguards) and 350 (confidentiality) (DLA Piper, updated 2 Feb 2026). These bind telecom providers, not app developers.
- [V-reg] Bill C.B. 24-17, "FSM Personal Data Protection Act of 2025", would cover only core National Government departments and agencies (s.103), not private businesses (bill text on cfsm.gov.fm).
- [V-sec] Committee report SCR 24-30 (2026) on the bill after hearings in Yap, Chuuk and Kosrae; no enactment found.
- No children's consent age for data, no breach-notice rule, no localisation rule found.

## Compact of Free Association with the US
- [K] FSM is a sovereign state in a Compact of Free Association with the US and uses the US dollar. US federal laws (COPPA, FTC Act, state privacy laws) do not apply as FSM domestic law. They still apply to you if you are a US-based developer.

## Kids and games
- [K] No kids' online or loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] No national VAT/GST. National 3% gross revenue tax on businesses operating in FSM (Rivermate; FSM tax code Title 54). The four states can levy their own sales taxes. No rule found making foreign digital sellers collect tax.
- [K] No consumer refund law for online sales found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No privacy law reaches private apps: follow the general rules in SKILL.md.
- Nothing to collect for tax at small scale.
- The pending bill is government-only; it won't change much for you even if passed.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] C.B. 24-17 (Personal Data Protection Act 2025, government agencies only) pending in Congress; no date.
