# Sri Lanka (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low now, Med from 2027 when the privacy law's main duties start.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, amended by Act No. 22 of 2025 (certified 30 Oct 2025). Main duties (Parts I and III) start 1 Jan 2027; the old 18 Mar 2025 date was withdrawn (readme.lk policy watch; dpa.gov.lk still shows the old date [V-reg, outdated]).
- [V-sec] Reaches overseas organisations that offer goods/services to people in Sri Lanka or monitor their behaviour.
- [V-sec] Regulator: Data Protection Authority (DPA), set up 2023. Breach-notice rules are still draft.
- [K] Child = under 16 for consent (parent needed). Not confirmed after the 2025 amendment.
- [K] Cross-border transfers allowed under adequacy / safeguards; data protection officer required for some.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game-time or loot-box law found. Gambling needs a licence.

## Selling
- [V-sec] 18% VAT on services supplied by non-residents through electronic platforms to people in Sri Lanka (SaaS, streaming, gaming...).
- [V-sec] Start date conflicts: postponed from 1 Oct 2025 to 1 Apr 2026 (Anrok); another source says 1 Jul 2026. A May 2026 VAT amendment bill exists (KPMG).
- [V-sec] Registration threshold: LKR 60M in 12 months or LKR 15M in 3 months (PayPro, search snippets). Small devs are below this.
- Practical: you are almost certainly under the threshold; a Merchant of Record covers it anyway.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] Online Safety Act No. 9 of 2024: 24-hour takedown orders, can require platforms to register. The Online Safety Commission is reported as not functioning / amendments under way; enforcement against small apps unlikely.
- [K] No AI law; a national AI strategy exists (draft).

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Treat users like GDPR users now so you're ready for 1 Jan 2027.
- Parent consent (or block) under 16.
- Have a report/takedown button for user content.
- Sales: no action below LKR 60M/yr.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] PDPA main obligations: 1 Jan 2027.
- [V-sec] Online Safety Act amendments: consultation 2025, not passed (as found).

## Unverified
- Exact non-resident VAT start date (Apr vs Jul 2026); child age after amendment; breach deadline.
