# Iceland (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app. Iceland is in the EEA, so GDPR applies as in the EU. This file only lists what Iceland adds.
Note: no regulator site (personuvernd.is, rsk.is) read; island.is and law-firm pages seen only as snippets.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Act 90/2018 on Data Protection and Processing of Personal Data implements GDPR; in force 15 Jul 2018. Regulator: Persónuvernd. (White & Case, Linklaters)
- [V-sec] Children can consent to online services from age 13 (the GDPR minimum); younger children need a parent. (island.is, Bird & Bird, Linklaters)
- [V-sec] Persónuvernd has fined: Reykjavik EUR 36,000 for the Seesaw school app (children's data). (Enforcement Tracker)
- [V-sec] Media Act amendment: media providers may not use children's data for commercial purposes (ads, profiling). (DataGuidance)
- [K] GDPR rules apply as usual: 72h breach notice, EU/EEA representative duty for foreign controllers (one in Iceland or elsewhere in the EEA).

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] A bill to set a 15-year minimum age for social media was put out for consultation 7 Jul to 7 Oct 2025. Outcome not confirmed; check before launch. (Digital Policy Alert)
- [K] No loot-box law found.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Non-resident B2C e-services: Icelandic VAT (24% standard, [K]) once Icelandic sales pass ISK 2M in a rolling 12 months; register through the VOES scheme at RSK. The EU OSS does NOT cover Iceland, so EU OSS registration does not help here. Returns every 2 months. (Stripe guide, Kintsugi, vatcalc)
- [K] EEA consumer law: 14-day withdrawal for distance sales, digital-content waiver allowed with consent.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] EU AI Act incorporation into the EEA Agreement not confirmed as of May 2026; Iceland has a national AI Action Plan 2024-2026. Plan as if it will apply. (regulations.ai, Chambers)
- [K] EEA Digital Services Act and e-commerce rules apply or are being incorporated; notice-and-takedown for user content.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Same as EU GDPR; parent consent under 13 for Iceland (the EU-wide safest default is 16).
- Selling direct: watch the ISK 2M threshold; otherwise a merchant of record covers it.
- No children's-data-based ads or profiling.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Under-15 social media age bill (outcome unknown). EU AI Act EEA incorporation pending (date unknown).
