# Tajikistan (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (weak privacy enforcement); Med only if you sell to Tajik consumers (VAT on e-services).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: no official Tajik source (adliya.tj, andoz.tj) was read; all lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Law "On Personal Data Protection" No. 1537 of 3 Aug 2018. A 2021 Communication Service decree sets security measures. (DLA Piper, Gratanet)
- [V-sec] Regulator unclear: DLA Piper names the Communication Service under the Government; another summary names the Agency of Innovation and Digital Technologies. (DLA Piper, CaseGuard)
- [V-sec] Consent (of the person or their legal representative) is the main basis; biometric data only with consent. No registration with the regulator needed. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] Cross-border transfer: free to countries with adequate protection; otherwise with consent, a treaty, or to protect rights, health or public order. (DLA Piper)
- [V-sec] No breach-notification duty. Fines up to about USD 1,700; enforcement described as "in its infancy". (DLA Piper, ConsentStack)
- [V-sec] Foreign reach: the law does not say whether it covers foreign controllers. (DLA Piper)
- [K] Age of majority 18; consent for a minor comes from the parent as legal representative. No children's-data age found.

## Kids and games
- [K] No loot-box or game-time law found. The state restricts content and blocks many sites; gambling is banned.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Since early 2021, foreign providers of electronic services to Tajik individuals must register and pay VAT (e-books, software, ads, hosting, games...). By Oct 2022 only 24 foreign firms had registered. (Trade.gov, PwC, VATCalc)
- [V-sec] Rate: 14% (from 1 Jan 2024), dropping to 13% on 1 Jan 2027. (VATCalc, PwC)
- [V-sec] Registration deadline unclear: 30 days (VATCalc) or 20 days (Tax Code Art. 193 per CABAR) after the first sale. No threshold found.
- [V-sec] Consumer law: Law "On Protection of Consumer Rights" (No. 438, 1997) plus a Law on Electronic Commerce. No online cooling-off rule found. (UNEP LEAP, Gratanet)
- Practical: use a Merchant of Record.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law. Tajikistan proposed regional (Central Asian) rules for AI in Dec 2025. (Intellinews)
- [K] Expect blocking of content critical of the government; moderate user content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Paid sales to Tajik consumers: VAT 14% (13% from 2027), or use a Merchant of Record.
- Get consent; parent consent for under-18s.
- Privacy enforcement against foreign apps is unlikely, but keep a basic privacy notice.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] VAT drops 14% to 13% on 1 Jan 2027. (VATCalc)

## Unverified
- Current regulator; whether the law reaches foreign apps; exact VAT registration deadline and threshold; any 2024-2026 amendments to the privacy law; consumer refund rules for digital goods.
