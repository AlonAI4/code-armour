# Bolivia (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Note: only search snippets read; no Bolivian official site opened. [V-sec] = news/aggregator text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law. A bill (PL 538/2025-2026, "Ley de Protección de Datos Personales") is in the Chamber of Deputies; an AGETIC-sponsored draft from 2024 was still pending in May 2026 (law.ungovr.org summary).
- [K] Constitution art. 130 gives a habeas data action; the 2011 Law 164 (telecom/ICT) and Law 453 (consumer) have scattered privacy rules.
- [K] Child consent, breach deadlines, localisation: none (no law).

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Bills on child online safety circulate in the Senate and Telecom regulator (ATT) in 2026; none confirmed in force.
- [K] No loot-box or age-rating rule found. Online gambling is restricted [K].

## Selling
- [V-sec] Government proposed extending 13% IVA to foreign digital services (Netflix, Google, Amazon...), with voluntary registration without a physical office and bank/card retention discussed; revenue estimate Bs 30-100 million (DPL News). Critics said it breaches tax principles. I could not confirm it passed or its date.
- [K] Bolivia's dollar shortage and capital controls make paying foreign sellers hard; many Bolivians cannot use foreign cards. Stripe does not support Bolivian businesses [K]; use app stores.
- [K] Consumer Law 453 (2013) applies to distance sales; details not checked.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] AI Bill 178/2024-2025 (transparency, human oversight, bans on mass biometrics and social scoring) is pending.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Basic privacy policy and minimal data; there is no binding privacy law today.
- Low priority market: don't build local tax set-up; use app stores or a Merchant of Record.
- If you have chat with kids, moderate it.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data protection bill PL 538/2025-2026, AI bill 178/2024-2025, foreign-digital-IVA proposal: all pending/unconfirmed.
