# The Gambia (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med on paper (new 2025 Act reaches foreign apps, parental consent under 18), Low in practice (regulator just starting).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act 2025: passed by the National Assembly 29 Sep 2025 (The Point, 6 Oct 2025), assented by President Barrow 7 Nov 2025 (captaincompliance, consentstack). The Point (17 Nov 2025) calls it "newly enacted". Gazetted commencement date not found.
- [V-sec] Regulator: the existing Information Commission (no new authority).
Lines marked [V-reg] below are from the "Data Protection and Privacy Bill, 2024" text on the National Assembly site. The enacted Act may differ:
- [V-reg] Reach: applies to processing outside The Gambia that relates to individuals in The Gambia (s.3(1)(c)). So yes, it reaches foreign developers. No small-business exemption; only personal/household use is excluded.
- [V-reg] Children: "child" = under 18 (s.2). Processing a child's data is lawful only with parent/guardian consent or another listed basis (s.7(5)); a child's own consent is not valid where it risks the child's best interests (s.7(4)). Extra safeguards for marketing/profiling and services aimed at children; plain-language notices (s.7).
- [V-reg] Breach: notify the Commission within 72 hours unless unlikely to cause high risk (s.32).
- [V-reg] Transfers abroad: allowed with an appropriate level of protection (the receiving country's law, or safeguards adopted by the Commission); the controller must assess it (s.37). No data-localisation rule found.
- [V-reg] Criminal offences: fines up to GMD 10 million for companies. The Commission can also issue warnings, orders, financial penalties and processing bans (s.42).
- [K] Cookies: no cookie-specific rule found; consent rules of the Act would cover non-essential tracking.

## Kids and games
- [K] Children's Act 2005: child = under 18. No loot-box or game-time rule found.

## Selling
- [K] VAT 15% (Gambia Revenue Authority). No rule found on VAT for foreign sellers of digital services.
- [V-sec] E-invoicing for VAT approved June 2026 (pilot first); foreign suppliers without Gambian VAT registration are not covered (Comarch, VATupdate).
- [K] Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2007; no online cooling-off period found.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Treat users under 18 as needing parent consent (or don't collect their data).
- Have a 72-hour breach plan and a clear privacy policy.
- Transfers to your host (e.g. US/EU) are fine if you can show safeguards; keep a note of your providers.
- Merchant of Record for paid sales.
