# Maldives (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app (no comprehensive privacy law in force yet; GST on digital services only above a turnover threshold).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory.
Note: mira.gov.mv and the Majlis/Gazette were not read for these rules; lines are secondary or memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law in force. A government Data Protection Bill passed first reading unanimously (66 MPs) and went to the ICT Committee in Jun 2026. It covers rights, controller/processor duties, DPOs, cross-border transfers and breach procedures, with the Information Commissioner (Right to Information Act) acting as Privacy Commissioner. (MMTV 22 Jun 2026, PSM News)
- [V-sec] Constitution Art. 24 protects privacy and private communications. (HRCM via Minivan archive, Constitution text)
- [K] Age of majority 18.

## Kids and games
- [V-sec] Child Rights Protection Act (Law 19/2019, in force Feb 2020). Section 33: no one may publish a child's personal information that could harm the child's dignity. (UNICEF, Edition.mv)
- [K] Gambling is banned (Islamic law). Avoid paid loot boxes with cash-out value.

## Selling
- [V-sec] GST: general rate 8%; tourism rate 17% since 1 Jul 2025. (Lookuptax, PayPro Global)
- [V-sec] From 1 Jul 2025 foreign sellers of digital services to Maldives residents must register with MIRA and charge 8% GST once turnover reaches MVR 1 million a year; must show TIN and the MIRA GST logo online. Sources conflict (one says no registration without a fixed place of business). (Lookuptax, PayPro Global; vatcompliance.co disagrees)
- [V-sec] From 1 Oct 2026 (Law 10/2026): non-resident booking platforms selling Maldives tourism (stays, transport, activities) must register and charge GST. (Lookuptax)
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Act (Act 12/2020): online sellers must state cancellation procedures; bans unfair terms, misrepresentation and bait ads. (UNEP LEAP, CTL Strategies)

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. Content that is "against Islam" or defamatory can be blocked; moderate user content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No privacy law yet, but follow general good practice; the new bill will likely bring breach and transfer rules.
- Don't publish children's personal details (Child Rights Protection Act s.33).
- Show your cancellation/refund policy clearly.
- GST only matters above MVR 1 million turnover a year (unless you sell tourism bookings).

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data Protection Bill at committee stage (Jun 2026); no enactment date. (MMTV)
- [V-sec] GST on non-resident tourism/booking supplies from 1 Oct 2026. (Lookuptax)

## Unverified
- Vendor sites claim a "Data Protection Act 2017"; not confirmed by any official or news source. Digital-services GST threshold and non-resident rules (sources conflict). Children's-data age in the bill.
