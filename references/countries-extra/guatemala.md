# Guatemala (researched 2026-10-05, by Claude Sonnet 5.5 with web search)
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here.
Note: only search snippets read; sat.gob.gt and congreso.gob.gt not opened. [V-sec] = news/aggregator text, [K] = memory.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] No comprehensive data protection law. Congress's Security Commission was still studying initiative 6464 (data protection and digital rights) on 16 Jul 2026; draft penalties of 6-8 years prison and Q50,000 fines for unlawful use of databases (Emisoras Unidas). Older Bill 6105 (Jun 2022) is also cited (DataGuidance).
- [K] Constitution art. 31 grants access to one's data; Access to Public Information Law covers public bodies; Consumer law (Decreto 6-2003) covers some privacy. No registration, breach rule or child age exists today.

## Kids and games
- [K] No game or loot-box law. No 2026 social-media age law found (search returned only other countries).

## Selling
- [V-sec] SAT said (in a Deloitte Tax Alert, 2021) that no law change is needed to charge IVA on services/digital goods from abroad, and planned voluntary registration of non-resident providers (Deloitte GT Alert 21-13). A later source says Guatemala has no dedicated non-resident digital-service regime, so cross-border B2C is outside the VAT net; payments to non-residents face a separate 15% income-tax withholding (Kintsugi/PayPro; secondary, check). IVA rate 12% [K].
- [K] Stripe does not support Guatemalan businesses; use app stores/Merchant of Record. Consumer Protection Law Decreto 6-2003 applies [K].

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found. [K] Cybercrime law is also pending (Iniciativa 6347 type), unconfirmed.

## What a small foreign dev realistically must do
- Short privacy policy, minimal data, deletion on request. Nothing is registered or filed.
- Sell through app stores or a Merchant of Record.
- Watch bill 6464.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Data protection initiative 6464 in committee (July 2026); no date.

## Unverified

- Final contents of 6464; child age; breach deadline.
- Any 2026 child online safety rule.
