# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Low for a small foreign-run app with a few users here (no data protection law in force; no digital-services VAT rule found).
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official law text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tracker, [K] memory. uwi.edu blocked automated reading (403); no official SVG law text was read.

## Privacy
- [V-sec] **No data protection law in force for private businesses.** The Privacy Act No. 18 of 2003 covers only public authorities and has never been commenced (no Gazette order as of Feb 2025, UWI; none found, offlist.me). No Privacy Commissioner.
- [V-sec] No data protection bill found before Parliament 2024-2026. A 2013 ICT law review mentioned privacy reform (Searchlight). One travel site claims a "Data Protection Act No. 16 of 2021": not confirmed anywhere official, likely wrong.

## Kids and games
- [K] No children's data, loot-box or game-time law. Age of majority 18. Gambling needs a local licence.

## Selling
- [V-sec] Consumer Protection Act No. 12 of 2020 (passed 14 Aug 2020) is in force; a minister said in Mar 2026 that "no refund" signs often break it (One News St Vincent). The 2019 draft had a "right to rescind or cancel agreement" (gov.vc draft).
- [V-sec] VAT 16% standard (11% hotels); registration above XCD 300,000 (Deloitte 2025). Imported services reportedly under reverse charge; no non-resident digital-services registration rule found (expanship).
- [K] Stripe does not support SVG-based businesses; selling to Vincentians from abroad works.

## AI, content and platforms
- [V-sec] No AI law found; CARICOM AI Policy Roadmap endorsed 18 Jun 2026 (policy only).
- [K] Cybercrime Act 2016 covers online harassment and illegal content.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- No data law to comply with, but keep a simple privacy policy and good security (users elsewhere still matter).
- Do not print "no refunds" in shop terms aimed at SVG users; offer a fair refund/cancel path.
- Watch for a digital-services VAT (Grenada passed one in 2026).

## Upcoming
