# Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville) (researched 2026-10-07, by Claude Opus 5.5 with web search)
General notes, not legal advice. See the disclaimer in README.md in this folder.
Severity: Med for a small foreign-run app: VAT on foreign digital sales from July 2026 with no threshold; data commission newly set up. Not the DRC: see `dr-congo.md` for Kinshasa.
Marks: [V-reg] regulator/official text, [V-sec] law firm/news/tax vendor, [K] memory. Law text read in the official gazette (sgg.cg).

## Privacy
- [V-reg] Law No. 29-2019 of 10 Oct 2019 on personal data protection (Journal officiel, 7 Nov 2019). [V-sec] In force (DLA Piper).
- [V-reg] Applies to controllers in Congo, and to controllers "établi ou non au Congo" that use means of processing on Congolese territory; those must name a representative in Congo. [K] A foreign app hosted abroad probably falls outside the "means" test, but Congolese users' phones may be argued to be "means".
- [V-reg] Breach: notify the commission within 72 hours where possible (Art. 74), unless no risk; tell users if high risk (DLA Piper [V-sec]).
- [V-reg] Fines XAF 1 million to 100 million, plus suspension or ban of processing.
- [V-sec] Processing must be declared to the commission; prior authorisation for sensitive data, which includes data on minors. Transfers only to countries with similar protection, with prior notice to the commission (DLA Piper).
- [V-sec] National Personal Data Protection Commission: creation bill approved by ministers Jul 2024; members presented by the ICT minister (ITWeb, 22 Jan 2026). Not known whether it is operational.
- [V-sec] Congo ratified the AU Malabo Convention (law promulgated 20 Aug 2020) (Osiris, Digital Business Africa).

## Kids and games
- [V-reg] "Digital majority" (Art. 62): a minor can consent alone to information-society services from age 16. Under 16, consent must be given jointly by the minor and a parent. Information for minors must be in plain language.
- [V-sec] A 20% "cybersecurity fee" was reported on online gaming and money transfers (Ecomatin). [K] Detail unconfirmed; real-money games need a licence anyway.

## Selling
- [V-sec] From 1 Jul 2026 (2024 Finance Law): 18% VAT on digital services supplied by non-residents to customers in Congo, B2C and B2B, no threshold; register on the ARPCE portal (tvadigital.arpce.cg); quarterly returns. Games, software, streaming, SaaS, e-learning all in scope (Fonoa, VATCalc).
- [V-sec] Comarch reports the start as 1 Aug 2026, so the start date differs between sources.
- [K] No online cooling-off right confirmed. Stripe doesn't support sellers in Congo; mobile money (MTN, Airtel) is common.

## AI, content and platforms
- [K] No AI law found.

## What a small foreign dev should consider
- Parent consent for under-16s (joint consent), clear simple wording for kids.
- 72-hour breach plan.
- Selling to Congolese users: Merchant of Record or app stores; direct sales mean ARPCE registration from sale 1.
- French privacy notice and a deletion route.

## Upcoming
- [V-sec] Digital-services VAT start: 1 Jul 2026 (Fonoa) or 1 Aug 2026 (Comarch); already started either way.
- [K] Data commission becoming operational: no date.
